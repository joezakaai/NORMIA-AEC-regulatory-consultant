## 5. 🔴 THE CENTRAL FINDING — combining the plots buys you no extra height, and the topography is the binding constraint

### 5.1 Combining gains nothing in height. This is the answer to the question as asked.

The provision that normally rewards a larger plot is **Art. 16(ج)**:

> كل (100)م² زيادة على الحد الأدنى للإفراز المقرر يمنح (35) سم بالارتفاع
> كل (1)م زيادة في الحد الأدنى المطلوب للارتدادات الجانبية والأمامية يمنح (30) سم بالارتفاع

*Every 100 m² above the prescribed minimum subdivision area grants 35 cm of height;
every 1 m of extra side/front setback grants 30 cm.*

On a naïve reading, 4,000 m² against a 2,000 m² minimum = 2,000 m² surplus = 20 × 35 cm =
**+7.00 m**. That is exactly the kind of number that gets designed against — and it is **wrong here**.

**Art. 16(هـ) — VERIFIED, gazette p.17:**

> تستثنى المناطق المنظمة بأحكام سكن أخضر أو سكن ريفي أو سكن زراعي أو المناطق المنظمة بأحكام
> خاصة **والقطع المخدومة بطرق تقل سعتها عن (20)م** من تطبيق أحكام الفقرات (ب) و(ج) و(د) من هذه المادة.

*Green, Rural and Agricultural Residential zones, areas zoned under special provisions,
**and plots served by roads less than 20 m wide**, are excluded from paragraphs (b), (c) and (d).*

**This plot is served by a 6 m road and a 10 m street. Both are far below 20 m.**

> [!danger] Therefore — and this holds **regardless of the zoning class**
> - ❌ **No height bonus for the extra 2,000 m²** (Art. 16(ج)(1) disapplied)
> - ❌ **No height bonus for extra setbacks** (Art. 16(ج)(2) disapplied)
> - ❌ **No high-building regime** (Art. 16(ب) disapplied — the 8-storey/30 m table is unavailable)
> - ❌ **The 60 m provision is irrelevant** (Art. 16(د) disapplied)
>
> The road-width trigger is independent of the zoning question. Even if the land turns out
> to be سكن أ, the 20 m test still fails and (ب)(ج)(د) still do not apply.

**Also disapplied — Art. 16(و):** the additional 4 m floor requires a street of **not less
than 14 m**. 6 m and 10 m both fail. ❌ No additional floor.

**So what does combining actually buy you?** A single 4,000 m² development parcel instead
of two 2,000 m² ones — a larger, better-proportioned footprint, one shared access strategy,
one set of services, and the ability to place the building on the workable part of the
slope instead of being forced to build twice on land that partly cannot take it. Those are
real gains. **Extra height is not among them.**

### 5.3 How height is measured here — Art. 15, applied

**Art. 15(أ)(1) — VERIFIED, gazette p.14:**

> يكون ارتفاع البناء هو المسافة العامودية من متوسط مناسيب هذه الطرق الى اعلى نقطة من السطح
> الإنشائي للبناء **ولا تعتمد الطرق التي تقل سعتها عن (8) امتار** أو الطريق التي تقل واجهة
> القطعة عليها عن (50%) من طول تلك الواجهة لاحتساب متوسط المنسوب.

*Height is the vertical distance from the **average of the levels of these roads** to the
highest point of the structural surface. **Roads less than 8 m wide are not relied upon**,
nor a road on which the plot's frontage is less than 50% of the length of that frontage.*

Applying it:

| Road | Width | Plot frontage | Counts toward the datum? |
|---|---|---|---|
| طريق 6 متر (north) | 6 m | 47.26 m | ❌ **No — under 8 m** |
| شارع 10 متر (south) | 10 m | 47.88 m (100% of that side) | ✅ **Yes** |

**⇒ The height datum is the average level of the 10 m street along the 47.88 m south frontage.**

**Art. 15(و) — VERIFIED, gazette p.15:** where the plot fronts a single road that has
multiple levels along it, height is computed from the **average level of that road**. The
south frontage runs from ≈ 0.0 at its west end to ≈ 12.0 at its east end, so:

> **Indicative datum ≈ +6.0** (survey datum)
> 🟡 **ASSUMPTION** — read off contours. **Must be recomputed from levelled spot heights
> along the 10 m street frontage before it is used for anything.**

> [!warning] Interpretive question to put to the Committee in writing
> Art. 15(أ)(2) says that where a plot fronts **more than one** road that fails the (1)
> tests, height is taken from the average of **those** roads. The natural reading is that
> (1) drops non-qualifying roads while a qualifying one survives, and (2) is a fallback
> only when they all fail. On that reading the 6 m road drops out here and the datum is the
> 10 m street alone.
>
> **This is the single most consequential interpretive question on this site.** The 6 m road
> sits on the high (north) side; including it would lift the datum by several metres and
> change the entire massing. **Get the Committee's position in writing before design.**

### 5.5 The three reliefs that make this site work

These are the levers. All three are in the regulation; none is discretionary guesswork.

**① Art. 15(ج) — measure from natural ground instead** (VERIFIED, p.14)

> إذا كان منسوب الأرض الطبيعية لقطعة الأرض أعلى من منسوب الطريق فيسمح بما يلي:
> 1. احتساب ارتفاع البناء من منسوب الأرض الطبيعية **شريطة أن لا يشتمل البناء على طوابق تسوية**.
> 2. استعمال أول طابق من البناء مواقف سيارات او خدمات **ويستثنى هذا الطابق من أحكام الارتفاع
>    وعدد الطوابق** المسموح بها في هذا النظام.

On the eastern half, natural ground (+12 to +15) is far above the datum (+6.0), so this
relief is **squarely available** and it is what converts the upper part of the plot from
unbuildable to buildable: 9 m measured **up from local natural ground**, plus a first floor
of parking/services that **does not count** toward either the 9 m or the 2-storey limit.

**Price:** no **طابق تسوية** (semi-basement) anywhere in that building.

**But note the distinction that saves the scheme** — Art. 15(ج)(1) excludes **طوابق تسوية**
only. A true **قبو (basement)** is a different creature under the Art. 2 definitions:

> **طابق القبو**: الطابق أو الطوابق التي تقع **تحت مستوى المنسوب الطبيعي لقطعة الأرض من الجهات
> جميعها** على أن لا يتجاوز سطحه الخرساني ارتفاع **(45) سم** عن منسوب الأرض الطبيعية أو متوسط
> منسوب الطريق المحاذي للقطعة **أيهما أدنى**، ويسمح بإقامته على **كامل حدود قطعة الأرض باستثناء
> الارتداد الأمامي**.
>
> **طابق التسوية**: الطابق أو الطوابق التي تقع تحت منسوب الطابق الأرضي ولا يتجاوز فيه منسوب
> سطحه الخرساني **متراً ونصف المتر** من متوسط منسوب الطريق.

And from the Art. 2 definitions of the ratios:
- **النسبة المئوية للبناء (coverage)** excludes **الأقبية**, car parking, projections, open
  passages and stairs, tanks, electrical rooms, light wells.
- **النسبة الطابقية (FAR)** excludes **طوابق التسوية والأقبية**, mechanical service floors,
  parking, projections, commercial mezzanines.

> [!tip] The key design move
> **Bury the uphill (eastern) side as a قبو**, not as a تسوية. A قبو is excluded from
> **both** coverage and floor area, may occupy the **entire plot except the front setback**,
> and — because it is not a تسوية — it does **not** forfeit the Art. 15(ج) relief.
> Restriction: Art. 38(أ) limits قبو use to **parking, RC water tanks, storage, heating/
> cooling plant and fuel tanks, electrical rooms**. It may **not** be habitable.

**② Art. 36(ب)(4) — ground floor as parking is excluded from height** (VERIFIED, p.33)

> يستثنى الطابق الأرضي المخصص لمواقف سيارات من الارتفاع المقرر للبناء ويسمح باستعمال ما لا
> يزيد على (25%) من مساحته خدمات عامة للمبنى.

Complementary to ①. Also **Art. 36(ب)(5)**: parking in the **تسوية** is excluded from the
coverage percentage (if you take the تسوية route instead of ①).

**③ Art. 33(د) — deck the setbacks** (VERIFIED, p.31)

> للجنة المختصة السماح بسقف الارتدادات على أن تُدرس كل حالة بحالتها **استناداً إلى مخطط طبوغرافي
> دقيق يُرفق مع الطلب** شريطة أن يكون السقف داعماً للشارع أو الجدار الاستنادي أو جهة قطع صخري،
> على أن يكون منسوب السقف دون مستوى الأرض الطبيعية في موقعه من الجهات جميعها.
> … **ج- إذا وقعت القطعة على أكثر من شارع يكون السقف جهة الشارع العلوي أو السفلي أو كليهما.**
> … **تستثنى مساحة الأسقف بالارتدادات من المساحة الإجمالية للبناء والمساحة الطابقية.**

This provision is written for exactly this situation:
- It **requires** an accurate topographic plan with the application — **you already have one,
  dated 13-6-2026, drawn for this combined plot.** That is a genuine procedural advantage.
- **This plot fronts two streets**, so the clause permitting decking on the **upper street
  side or the lower street side or both** is available.
- Front-setback decking must support the street/retaining wall/rock cut, its length must not
  exceed the building façade length on the street, and its level must follow the adjoining
  street level and **in no case exceed 45 cm**.
- Use beneath is limited to services: parking, vehicle passages, water/diesel/gas wells and
  tanks, central heating/cooling, electrical rooms, transformer stations, non-commercial storage.
- **Deck area is excluded from both total built area and floor area.**

**④ Art. 16(ز) — relief from the coverage percentage** (VERIFIED, p.17)

> للمجلس الموافقة على إقامة أبنية دون التقيد بالنسبة المئوية المسموح بها في الجدول … شريطة تقديم
> مخططات لكامل البناء المقترح والتقيد بالنسبة الطابقية والارتدادات المقررة.

The Council may waive the coverage percentage provided full plans are submitted and the FAR
and setbacks are respected. On a 25% slope this is worth pursuing: it lets you **spread the
building out lower and wider** — terraced, following the contours — instead of stacking it
up against a ceiling you cannot reach on the high side.

## 8. Controls common to both scenarios — all verified

### Roof floor — Art. 48(أ), p.46
1. Area ≤ **50%** of the floor it sits on
2. Height ≤ **3.5 m** above that floor's slab level
3. Setbacks on all sides ≥ **half** the prescribed setbacks (Scenario A: 4 / 3 / 3), **excluding
   the stair house and lift**

**Art. 48(ب)** — the additional طابق سطح on the front façade is available **only in residential
zones أ، ب، ج، د**. ❌ Not available here under either scenario.

### Basement and semi-basement — Art. 2 definitions + Art. 38
- **قبو**: below natural ground **on all sides**; concrete surface ≤ **45 cm** above natural
  ground **or** average adjoining road level, **whichever is lower**; may occupy the **whole
  plot except the front setback**; **excluded from coverage and FAR**; use limited to parking,
  RC water tanks, storage, heating/cooling and fuel tanks, electrical rooms — **not habitable**.
- **تسوية**: concrete surface ≤ **1.5 m** above the average road level; excluded from FAR;
  parking within it excluded from coverage (Art. 36(ب)(5)); **but its presence forfeits the
  Art. 15(ج) natural-ground relief**.
- **Art. 15(د)**: the ground floor slab may be raised a maximum of **2 m** above natural ground
  or road level where there is **no** basement or semi-basement, and provided the overall height
  limit is respected.

### Parking — Art. 36, p.33–34
- Rate for **المناطق السكنية (أ، ب، ج، ريفي، زراعي)**: **one space per 300 m² of total built
  area, or one space per apartment, whichever is the greater number.**
- **Art. 36(ب)(1):** no building may be licensed unless parking for the **full permitted
  building on the plot** has been provided — **regardless of how much you are licensing now**.
  This bites on any phased scheme.
- **Art. 36(ب)(2):** if fewer spaces are provided, the Committee may licence only the area or
  apartment count matching them, **and no further area may ever be licensed**.
- **Art. 36(ب)(3):** a part space counts as a whole space.
- **Art. 36(ج):** parking in the **front setback is not permitted in residential zones**.
- **Art. 36(د):** open parking **is** permitted in side and rear setbacks subject to four
  conditions (clear width, front setback kept clear behind, entrance opening ≥ the clear
  width, manoeuvring within the plot).

At Scenario A's 2,700 m²: **9 spaces** by area — or one per apartment if that gives more.

### Fences and setback fill — Art. 33(ب), p.31
- Fence height from **natural ground**: **1.5 m front**, **2 m rear and sides**
- **Fill (طمم) in the setbacks must not exceed 1 m above natural ground at its location**
  🔴 On a 25% slope this is a hard constraint: **you cannot simply platform the setbacks**.
  The Art. 33(د) decking route (§5.5 ③) is the compliant alternative to filling.
- Fill **is** permitted beneath any open parking space in the side setback in residential zones,
  and at the front boundary to meet the street entry level.

### Projections — Art. 39, p.28
> لا يسمح بأي بروز في الحالات التي يكون فيها عرض الشارع (12) متراً أو أقل

**Both streets are 12 m or less.** ❌ **No commercial projections / no balconies overhanging
the street or the front setback.** Architectural projections of up to **60 cm** within the
setbacks remain available (Art. 39(ب)).

### Subdivision relief — Art. 13(أ), p.12
The Committee may reduce subdivision restrictions by up to **10%** where the land is irregular
in shape, carries an existing building, has had more than a quarter expropriated, or the
subdivision is between co-owners — **and up to 15% in agricultural, rural or green residential
zones**. A relief valve worth knowing about, though the combined plot needs no such relief.

## 9. Design strategy — what actually follows from all this

Under **Scenario A**, with the levels as surveyed:

1. **Place the building mass on the lower, western half.** That is where the ceiling at
   ≈+15.0 leaves real headroom and where the floor count, not the height, binds.
2. **Terrace with the contours, not against them.** The footprint runs north–south; the fall
   runs east–west. A long, narrow, stepped building parallel to the contours works with the
   site; a compact block across them does not.
3. **Take the coverage, not the height.** You are permitted 1,080 m² of footprint inside a
   2,428 m² envelope, and you cannot go up. Spread out. If the scheme wants more footprint
   than 27%, **Art. 16(ز)** is the route — full plans, FAR and setbacks respected.
4. **Bury the uphill side as a قبو, never as a تسوية.** The قبو is excluded from coverage and
   FAR, may run to the plot boundary except in the front setback, and preserves the Art. 15(ج)
   relief. The تسوية forfeits it.
5. **If any mass must go on the upper (eastern) part, take Art. 15(ج) there** — measure from
   natural ground, and use the first floor for parking/services so it drops out of both the
   height and the floor count.
6. **Use Art. 33(د) decking on both street ends** to recover usable service area that counts
   against neither coverage nor floor area, and to handle the level change at the street edges
   within the 45 cm rule — rather than filling, which is capped at 1 m.
7. **Design the parking for the full entitlement from day one** (Art. 36(ب)(1)), even if
   construction is phased.
8. **Retaining structures are unavoidable.** Budget the geotechnical work early; Art. 33(د)
   decking is conditioned on the structure supporting the street, the retaining wall or the
   rock cut.
