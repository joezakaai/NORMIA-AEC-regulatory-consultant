# Qatar Building Permit Law and Process

> [!info] [[NORMIA]] · [[Qatar]] · [[Building Codes]] · [[Authority Directory]]

## The statute

**Law No. 4 of 1985 on the Organization of Buildings**, amended by **Law No. 8 of 2014**
("Amending Certain Provisions of Law No. 4 of 1985 on the Organisation of Buildings").

**No newer replacement building law was found.** Law 4/1985 as amended appears to remain the
governing instrument.

The 2014 amendment reportedly raised penalties for unpermitted works and introduced controls on
exterior building colour ("limit the exterior colour of buildings to those pre-approved by the
municipality") — but this rests on a **third-party news source**, and the statutory text could not
be fetched. **Treat the article-level content as UNVERIFIED.**

Al Meezan (the Ministry of Justice legal portal) hosts the free full text in Arabic and English at
`almeezan.qa` — reachable from an unrestricted connection.

## Who issues the permit

Verbatim from the official Hukoomi service page:

> the **"Development and Building Permits Section of the Technical Affairs Department at the
> Ministry of Municipality"**

Two practical points confirmed on the same page:

- Applications are submitted **through a municipality consultant** — **individuals cannot
  self-file**.
- For the "small additions" service, additions must not exceed **100 square metres**.

## The technical stack

There is **no single instrument called "the Qatar Building Code"**. The stack is:

| Layer | Instrument |
| --- | --- |
| Statute | **Law 4/1985 as amended by Law 8/2014** — permit authority and offences |
| Planning / development control | **[[QNMP Zoning Regulation Framework]]** — zone regulations, overlays, guidelines |
| Construction technology | **[[Qatar Construction Specifications (QCS)]]** |
| Fire & life safety | **[[QCDD Civil Defence Technical Requirements Guide]]** — separate mandatory approval |
| Building envelope / energy | **[[KAHRAMAA Energy and Water Conservation Code]]** — enforced through the electricity connection |
| Utilities & roads | KAHRAMAA · Ashghal · Ooredoo |

## Who may sign and submit drawings

**Engineer and consulting-office registration is a Ministry of Municipality regime**, under
**Law No. (19) of 2005**, administered by the Engineers & Consulting Offices Accrediting &
Classifying Committee. Verbatim from the official MME document:

> "All those who work at different engineering consultancy offices and other engineering fields
> should be registered at the engineers register, excluding those who work at the Government sector."
>
> "Engineer shall mention his name and registration number on all drawings, designs and reports
> prepared by him."

Grades: Trainee · Third Class · Second Class · First Class · Expert. This is the **UPDA**
registration that Civil Defence submission guidelines also require.

> [!warning] Two separate registration regimes — do not conflate
> **MoM / UPDA** — engineers and consulting offices: *who may sign and submit drawings.*
> **MOI / QCDD** — fire protection companies: *who may supply, install and maintain fire systems.*
> There is no evidence of a separate QCDD registration for **design** consultants.

## System modernisation

The Ministry of Municipality operates an **electronic Building Permit System (e-BP)**, amended
from Q1 2024, and has inaugurated an **AI-powered building permit issuance system**. The system's
product name and launch date could not be confirmed — the official news pages did not render.

A **"Requirements and Services Guidebook for the Building Permits System"** was reported issued in
August 2022, but **no public download exists** — treat as a counter-issued/internal document.

## Separate permitting jurisdictions

**Qatar Free Zones** permit development independently under **Law No. (34) of 2005 as amended by
Decree-Law No. (21) of 2017**. Verbatim from the QFZ explanatory note:

> "A Building Permit will be compulsory for carrying out any Development"
> "A Free Zone Entity cannot Occupy the Development unless the Authority has issued a Final
> Certificate."

The note makes **no reference to the Ministry of Municipality**. The same carve-out logic applies
to **Lusail, Qetaifan Island North, Msheireb and Education City** — see
[[Lusail — Regulatory Hierarchy and Mandatory Status]] for the worked example of how a
master-developer regime sits alongside municipal permitting.
