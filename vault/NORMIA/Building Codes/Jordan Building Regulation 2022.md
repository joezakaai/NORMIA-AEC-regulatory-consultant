# Jordan Building Regulation 2022

> [!info] [[NORMIA]] · [[Jordan]] · [[Building Codes]] · [[Authority Directory]]
> **نظام رقم (1) لسنة 2022 — نظام الأبنية وتنظيم المدن والقرى**
> Issued by royal order of **King Abdullah II ibn Al Hussein** under **Article 31 of the
> Constitution**, on a decision of the Council of Ministers. **49 pages.**
> Read together with **[[Jordan Amendment Regulation 12 of 2025]]** as one regulation.

This is the principal development-control instrument for Jordan — the equivalent in function of a
combined zoning ordinance and building regulation. It is made under the **Town, Village and
Building Planning Law (قانون تنظيم المدن والقرى والأبنية)**.

## The measurement definitions that govern everything else

> [!important] These definitions decide what counts. Read them before applying any table.

**المساحة الإجمالية للبناء — Total built area**
The sum of the horizontal projections of the building or buildings, **excluding**: basement floors,
commercial mezzanines, mechanical services floors, the area of architectural projections, car
parking, passages and open stairs, water wells, collection pits, fuel tanks, water tanks,
electricity rooms and their generators, light wells, mechanical ventilation voids, the lift machine
room roof, and the last-floor stair roof.

**المساحة الطابقية — Floor area**
The sum of the **roofed** horizontal projections of all floors of the building, **excluding**:
semi-basement floors, basements, commercial mezzanines, mechanical services floors, commercial
projections, architectural projections, car parking, passages and open stairs, water wells,
collection pits, light wells, mechanical ventilation voids, the lift machine room roof, and the
last-floor stair roof.

**النسبة الطابقية — Floor area ratio (FAR)**
The ratio of the **floor area** of the building to the area of the land on which it stands.

**النسبة المئوية للبناء — Coverage percentage**
The area of the **largest horizontal projection** of the building to the area of the land on which
it stands, **excluding** commercial projections, architectural projections, car parking, basements,
passages and open stairs, water wells and collection pits.

**البناء — Building**
Any construction, whether of stone, concrete, clay, iron, timber or any other material for building
purposes, and including excavations, foundations, walls, roofs, chimneys, verandas, corbelling and
cornices, or any projection or any part of the building or anything attached to it, or any wall,
fence, enclosure or other construction delimiting or enclosing any land, yard or water well.

**السدة التجارية — Commercial mezzanine**
A secondary floor forming part of a commercial premises, directly connected to it, accessible only
through the commercial premises connected to it.

**الشرفة — Balcony**
Any part of a building open on one or more sides and permanently exposed to air and natural light.

## Temporary buildings — Article 35

Temporary buildings are permitted for: storage of building materials, workers' accommodation,
guard rooms and temporary offices on the construction site during the works; tents for public
occasions and cultural and recreational activities and similar uses approved by the committee for a
defined period; and **mobile food trucks in commercial and mixed-use zones**, subject to obtaining
the necessary approvals.

## Design Consequence

**The exclusion lists are where the design value sits.** Basements, car parking, open stairs,
architectural projections, plant rooms, tanks and light wells all fall outside both the total built
area and the floor area. A scheme that pushes parking and services below ground or into the
permitted setback structures buys usable area at no cost against FAR or coverage.

**Coverage is measured on the largest horizontal projection, not the ground floor.** A cantilevered
upper floor wider than the ground floor sets the coverage figure. Check the widest level, not the
base.

**The committee, not the Council, is the day-to-day authority.** Most licensing decisions sit with
the local or district planning committee; the Higher Planning Council handles specific reserved
matters such as coverage relief under Article 16(g) and residential suburb projects.
