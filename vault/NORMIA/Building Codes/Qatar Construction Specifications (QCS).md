# Qatar Construction Specifications (QCS)

> [!info] [[NORMIA]] · [[Qatar]] · [[Building Codes]] · [[Authority Directory]]
> The national technical specification. **This is the closest thing Qatar has to a building code
> for construction technology** — there is no separate "Qatar Building Code" instrument.

> [!danger] Two editions are live at once, with different legal weight
> | Edition | Instrument | Date | Legal character |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> | **QCS 2014** | **Minister of Environment Decision No. 210/2014**, dated **4 February 2015** | eff. 2015 | **Mandatory Qatari technical regulation** |
> | **QCS 2024** | **Minister of Commerce & Industry Decision No. 15/2024**, gazetted 17 Mar 2024 | eff. **18 March 2024** | **OPTIONAL Qatari standard** — "دليل فني استرشادي" (technical guidance guide) |
>
> **No evidence was found that Decision 15/2024 repealed Decision 210/2014.** Ashghal was still
> publishing QCS 2014-referenced Standard Detail Drawings with modification dates to **June 2026**.
> **Establish which edition governs contractually on every project.**

## Legal basis — verbatim

From the official Ashghal tender specification GTC-134:

> "Qatar Construction Specifications (QCS2014) prepared by the Qatar General Organization for
> Standards and Metrology (QGOSM) **approved by Minister of Environment's Decision No.210/2014,
> dated 4th February 2015**"

The Al Meezan legal portal indexes MD 15/2024 as approving QCS 2024 "as an **optional** Qatari
standard specification" (اختيارية).

Note the issuing ministry changed between editions: **Environment (2014) → Commerce and Industry
(2024)**, tracking the standards body's move.

## Interim Advice Notes (IANs) — Ashghal only

IANs amend QCS **for Ashghal/PWA projects only**. Verbatim from PWA IAN 013/14 Rev A1:

> "Whilst IANs shall be read in conjunction with the Qatar Highway Design Manual (QHDM), the Qatar
> Traffic Manual (QTM) and the Qatar Construction Specifications (QCS), and may incorporate
> amendments or additions to these documents, **they are not official updates** to the QHDM, QTM,
> QCS or any other standards."

**IAN 013/14 Rev A1 (April 2016)** amends Sections 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 19 and 21. The IAN series runs
to at least No. 100. **No consolidated official IAN index page was found** — check the Ashghal
Services Library directly.
