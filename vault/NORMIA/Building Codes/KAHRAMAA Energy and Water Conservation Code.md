## Enforcement — verbatim

> "Buildings failing to comply with minimum insulation requirements will not be considered for
> supply of electricity."

This is enforced **through the electricity connection**, not through the building permit. A
non-compliant building can be built and then refused power. It is therefore effectively
non-negotiable regardless of what the permit authority accepts.

**Scope:** all air-conditioned new buildings and additions to existing buildings — residential,
commercial, governmental, industrial.

## Building envelope — verbatim

### Opaque envelope, maximum U-value

| Element | Maximum U-value |
| --- | --- |
| **Roof** | "0.437 W/m2 ºC (0.077 Btu/h ft2 ºF)" |
| **External Wall** | "0.568 W/m2 ºC (0.100 Btu/h ft2 ºF)" |

### Glazing — banded by Window Wall Ratio

| Window Wall Ratio | Maximum U-value | Maximum SHGC |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **5 – 40%** | "3.30 (0.58) W/m2 ºC" | **"0.3"** |
| **Above 40%** | "2.10 (0.36) W/m2 ºC" | **"0.25"** |
| **Showrooms** | "double glazing" · "2.10 (0.36) W/m2 ºC" | "0.3" |

## Design Consequence

**The WWR banding is a genuine design threshold, and it is a step change, not a gradient.** Cross
from 40% to 41% glazing and both the U-value and the SHGC requirement tighten at once — U-value
from 3.30 to 2.10, SHGC from 0.30 to 0.25. That is the difference between a standard double-glazed
unit and a high-performance selective coating, across the whole façade.

So the façade decision is binary and should be made deliberately at concept:

- **Stay at or below 40% WWR** and specify to the easier band, or
- **Go above 40%** and price the higher-specification glass from the outset.

Sliding from 38% to 43% during design development without re-specifying the glass is a classic
route to a refused electricity connection.

The **roof U-value of 0.437** is materially tighter than the wall at 0.568 — expect substantial
roof insulation build-up, which interacts with parapet height and any overall height limit
(see [[Qatar R1 — Low Density Residential Zone]], where 13 m includes the parapet).

## Interpretation Notes

The values above were quoted verbatim from the fetched official PDF but extracted through a
summarising layer from a 29-page document. **Verify against the source PDF before using them in a
compliance submission** — particularly the WWR banding, which reads as two bands (5–40%, >40%)
with **no stated treatment below 5%**.
