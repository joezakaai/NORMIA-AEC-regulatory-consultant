# Jordan Amendment Regulation 12 of 2025

> [!info] [[NORMIA]] · [[Jordan]] · [[Building Codes]] · [[Jordan Building Regulation 2022]]
> **نظام رقم (12) لسنة 2025 — نظام معدل لنظام الأبنية وتنظيم المدن والقرى**
> Signed **29/1/2025** by HRH **Prince Ali bin Al Hussein, Regent**, under Article 31 of the
> Constitution, on the Council of Ministers decision of 29/1/2025.
> Official Gazette **pages 835–837**.

## Article 1 — Reading and commencement

> "يُسمى هذا النظام (نظام معدل لنظام الأبنية وتنظيم المدن والقرى لسنة 2025) **ويقرأ مع النظام رقم
> (1) لسنة 2022** المشار إليه فيما يلي بالنظام الأصلي **نظاماً واحداً** ويعمل به من تاريخ نشره في
> الجريدة الرسمية."

It is read **as one regulation** with Regulation No. 1 of 2022, and takes effect from the date of
publication in the Official Gazette.

## Article 2 — amends Article 8(د)(8) of the original

Item (8) of paragraph (د) of Article 8 is **repealed and replaced** with:

> "8 — ما هو مسموح به في الفقرتين (أ) و (ج) من هذه المادة **شريطة الحصول على الموافقات البيئية
> اللازمة**."
>
> "What is permitted under paragraphs (a) and (c) of this Article, **subject to obtaining the
> necessary environmental approvals**."

## Article 3 — adds Articles 10(هـ) and 10(و)

**(هـ)** The provisions and conditions prescribed by the Council for licensing **residential suburb
projects (مشاريع الضواحي السكنية)** may **not be amended except with the Council's prior approval**,
and subject to the approval of the **general assembly of the union of owners** of residential suburb
projects.

**(و)** Except for **renewable energy projects**, the street serving the project land must be **not
less than 12 metres** wide.

> [!tip] A new hard gate on project access
> The 12 m minimum street width is a **new access threshold** for projects, with a single carve-out
> for renewable energy. Read it alongside the **20 m road test** in Article 16(e) of the original
> and the **14 m** street requirement for the additional floor in Article 16(f) — Jordan now has
> three distinct road-width thresholds that each unlock something different. Establish the serving
> road width before any feasibility conclusion.

## Article 4 — adds Article 41(ج)(9)

> "9 — **تأمين سارية علم** وعلى أن تحدد مواصفاتها وأبعادها بموجب تعليمات يصدرها الوزير لهذه الغاية."
>
> "Provision of a **flagpole**, its specifications and dimensions to be determined by instructions
> issued by the Minister."

A new mandatory building requirement. The specification is left to ministerial instructions — obtain
them before detailing.

## Article 5 — amends Article 52(أ): the settlement window

Item (1) is retained. Items **(2), (3) and (4)** are added:

### (2) Licensing of existing unlicensed buildings

Notwithstanding this regulation, the competent committee may license **existing buildings erected
from 1/1/2017 up to 31/12/2024**, subject to all of:

| # | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| أ | Submission of engineering plans **certified by the Jordan Engineers Association** (نقابة المهندسين الأردنيين) |
| ب | Submission of an approved certificate proving the building's **structural safety** |
| ج | The building must **not encroach** on others' land, State Treasury land, or the road |
| د | Encroachment in **setbacks must not exceed 50%**, and encroachment on the remaining provisions must not exceed **25%** of the prescribed provisions |
| هـ | The building use must **conform with the use prescribed** for the plot |

### (3) Fees

For licensing under item (2), the competent planning committee collects **50% of the encroachment
fees** prescribed in **Article 20**, and **50% of the parking in-lieu payment (بدل المواقف)**
prescribed in **Article 36(ح)**.

### (4) Deadline

> "تنتهي مهلة الترخيص للأبنية المنصوص عليها في البند (2) من هذه الفقرة بتاريخ **31/12/2025**."

> [!danger] This provision has LAPSED
> The settlement window in Article 52(أ)(2) **closed on 31 December 2025**. As at the date of this
> record (**16 September 2026**) it is **no longer available**.
>
> An existing unlicensed building erected between 1/1/2017 and 31/12/2024 that did not obtain a
> licence before the deadline **cannot now be regularised under this provision**. Do not advise a
> client that this route is open without first confirming with the Ministry whether the deadline
> was extended by a later regulation — extensions of such windows are common in Jordan, but
> **no extending instrument was supplied with these documents and none has been verified.**

## Design Consequence

Three of the five amendments are procedural or transitional. The two that change design decisions
are:

**The 12 m serving-street minimum (Article 10(و))** — a project on land served by a narrower street
is not licensable at all, renewable energy excepted. This is a site-selection test, not a design
one, and it applies before anything else in the regulation is reached.

**The flagpole requirement (Article 41(ج)(9))** — minor, but it is a mandatory provision with
specifications set separately by ministerial instruction. Get the instruction; do not guess
dimensions.
