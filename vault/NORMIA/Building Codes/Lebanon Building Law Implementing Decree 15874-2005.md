## The controls that shape a Lebanese building

| Control | Rule | Article |
|---|---|---|
| **Street envelope** | vertical **H = 2.5 × L** (minimum 15 m), then inclined **2 : 5** | 7 |
| **Court envelope** | vertical **H = 5 × E**, then inclined **1 : 5** | 7 |
| **Legal court** | **≥ 30 m²** AND must contain a **4.50 × 5.50 m** rectangle | 7 |
| **Room depth** | **P ≤ 5 × H** (clear height); **L ≤ 11 × a**; **f ≥ S / 10** | 9 |
| **Balcony projection** | **0 m** where the street is under 9 m; **1.05 m** at 9 m or more | 8 |
| **Independence** | **D ≥ 2 × R**, minimum **4.50 m** | 11 |
| **Parking** | by use class — residential **1 car per 100 m²** | 15 |
| **Clear height** | **2.80 m** habitable (horizontal ceiling); maximum **5.75 m** | 17 |

Above all of these sits the zone's own maximum height, which the envelope can never exceed:
> «مع عدم إمكانية تجاوز الارتفاع الأقصى المحدد في نظام المنطقة التي يقع فيها العقار…»
