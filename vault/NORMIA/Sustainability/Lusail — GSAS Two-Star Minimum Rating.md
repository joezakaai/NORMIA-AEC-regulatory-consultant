# Lusail — GSAS Two-Star Minimum Rating

> [!info] [[NORMIA]] · [[Authority Directory]] · [[Qatar]] · Discipline: [[Sustainability]]
> Source: `Qetaifan Island North District — Planning & Design Guidelines, Residential & Mixed-Use Plots` — REV 2, May 2023, p.27, 32

## Requirement

**§1.6.4, p.32 — GSAS in Lusail:**

> Lusail City is committed to the principles of sustainability and green buildings. As such,
> **all projects in Lusail must attain a GSAS two-star rating** under the Gulf Organization for
> Research & Development's (GORD) GSAS rating system.
>
> All projects should be conceived as "green" buildings early on in the design process …
> **Demonstration of this is required in all stages of CAC's development review and approval
> process.**
>
> GORD administers GSAS and is the entity that determines a building's star rating. GORD works
> hand-in-hand with CAC during the development review process and **should be consulted early
> on** to determine requirements for the star-rating the project is seeking to achieve.

**§1.5.2, p.27 — Sustainable Design:**

> All buildings and landscape areas are expected to meet GSAS requirements of GORD and Lusail
> City … **Where proposals better these minimums and can demonstrate significant sustainable
> improvements the Master Developer may offer incentives including GFA gains.** These will be
> considered on a project by project basis.

## Value / Criterion

| Parameter | Value | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Minimum GSAS rating | **2 star** | Mandatory for all Lusail projects |
| Rating body | GORD | Determines the star rating |
| Demonstration required at | **All CAC review stages** | Not only at completion |
| Exceeding the minimum | May earn incentives **including GFA gains** | Discretionary, case by case |

## Design Consequence

GSAS two-star is a hard gate, and it must be **demonstrated at every review stage** — so it
cannot be deferred to a post-DC-2 certification exercise. Appoint the GSAS consultant before
concept design is fixed: several GSAS criteria (orientation, envelope, shading, water strategy)
are decided by the massing and are expensive to recover later.

The commercially significant sentence is the last one. Exceeding the minimum **may earn GFA
gains** — and since FAR is the binding constraint on villa plots in this masterplan (see
[[QIN Villa Type B — FAR and Plot Coverage]]), additional GFA is the most valuable concession
available. Where a client wants more house, the sustainability route is worth pricing against
simply accepting the cap.

## Interpretation Notes

The GSAS **scheme and version are not stated** in the Design Guidelines, nor is the applicable
typology (GSAS Design & Build, residential/villa scheme). GSAS has been revised since the DG's
May 2023 edition. Confirm the scheme and version with GORD before setting targets.

Any GFA gain is discretionary — "may offer", "considered on a project by project basis". It is
an **ASSUMPTION** until confirmed in writing by the Master Developer and must not be carried
into a buildable envelope calculation.
