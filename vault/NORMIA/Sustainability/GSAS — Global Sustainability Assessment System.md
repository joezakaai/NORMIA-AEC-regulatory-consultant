# GSAS — Global Sustainability Assessment System

> [!info] [[NORMIA]] · [[Qatar]] · [[Sustainability]] · [[Authority Directory]]
> Issued by **GORD**, a research organisation — **not a government authority**. GSAS binds where a
> master developer, client or tender imposes it.

> [!warning] There is no national GSAS mandate
> No Qatari law, ministerial decision or regulation imposing GSAS nationally was found. What is
> confirmed: **Lusail City mandates a 2-star minimum**, and GSAS was adopted as the **Gulf
> Standard GSO 3000:2025** by the GCC Standardisation Organisation. **Adoption as a GSO standard
> does not by itself make it mandatory in Qatar.**

## Editions — cite the manual, not a global version number

**GSAS is in its 4th Edition**, but the dates differ by scheme:

| Scheme | Title on cover | Edition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Construction Management | *"GSAS 2019 CONSTRUCTION MANAGEMENT: ASSESSMENT MANUAL FOR BUILDINGS AND INFRASTRUCTURE - 4th Edition"* | 4th Ed, **2019** |
| Operations | *"GSAS 2019 Operations: Assessment & Guidelines Manual for Existing Buildings"* | 4th Ed, **Issue 2, 2019** |
| Design & Build (Parks) | *"GSAS DESIGN & BUILD ASSESSMENT MANUAL FOR PARKS"* | 4th Ed, **2025** |

**The Design & Build manuals were reissued in 2025 while still branded 4th Edition.** There is no
"GSAS 2024" or "GSAS v2.1". **Cite the specific manual, edition, issue and year.**

## Three schemes — three different rating vocabularies

> [!danger] Do not conflate them — "2-star" is a Design & Build concept only

### Design & Build — **stars**

**16 schemes:** Districts · Neighborhoods · Parks · Offices · Commercial · Retail · Homes ·
Residential (multiunit) · Hospitality · Sports · Education · Healthcare · Mosques · Railways ·
Light Industry · plus Interior (fit-outs), Renovation, Bespoke, Energy Center SEER.

**8 categories — verbatim:** *"Urban Connectivity, Site, Energy, Water, Materials, Indoor
Environment, Cultural & Economic Value and Management & Operations."*

**Two-stage certification — verbatim:** provisional via *"Letter of Conformance (LOC) following the
design phase"*, then *"Conformance to Design Audit (CDA) to qualify for the final certification."*
Requires appointed GSAS Service Providers holding a **GSAS Service Provider License, GSAS-D&B CGP
License, and GSAS Energy Assessment Certificate**.

> [!warning] Star thresholds are UNVERIFIED
> Stars 1–6 demonstrably exist (GORD has awarded "GSAS 4 Star" ratings), but **the official
> score-to-star conversion table could not be obtained** — the Building Typologies Assessment
> Manual, Technical Guide, Interiors manual and scoring sheets all returned **HTTP 403**.
> **Do not quote D&B star thresholds as fact.**

### Construction Management — **Classes**

8 categories: UC Urban Considerations · S Site · E Energy · W Water · M Materials ·
**OE Outdoor Environment** · **SD Socio-Cultural Dimension** · MO Management & Operations.

| Score | Rating |
| --- | --- |
| Below 0.5 | Certification Denied |
| 0.5 to <1.0 | Class D |
| 1.0 to <1.5 | Class C |
| 1.5 to <2.0 | Class B |
| 2.0 to <2.5 | Class A |
| 2.5 and above | Class A* |

### Operations — **metals**

6 categories: E Energy · W Water · IE Indoor Environment · WM Waste Management ·
FM Facility Management · EPA Environmental Policy & Awareness.

| Score | Rating |
| --- | --- |
| X < 0.5 | Certification Denied |
| 0.5 ≤ X < 1.0 | Bronze |
| 1.0 ≤ X < 1.5 | Silver |
| 1.5 ≤ X < 2.0 | Gold |
| 2.0 ≤ X < 2.5 | Platinum |
| X ≥ 2.5 | Diamond |

Four pathways: Standard Scheme (max Gold) · Premium Scheme (max Diamond) · Energy Neutral Mark ·
Healthy Building Mark.

Both CM and Operations use a criterion scoring scale of **0 to 3**, where *"level 0 refers to
'evidence not acceptable' or 'requirements not attained'"*.

**Category weights are not published** — only the statement that *"each criterion has an associated
weight based on the relative environmental, social and economic impact"*. Weights presumably sit in
the 403-blocked manuals.

## Where GSAS is mandatory

**Lusail City — confirmed.** Verbatim from the official Lusail document:

> "Lusail City mandates that all projects constructed in the city should attain a minimum of
> GSAS 2 star rating."

Document: **"Lusail City - GSAS 2 Star Rating - Guidelines", V1.0, November 2014**, issued by GORD
and filed under **Building Permit Application Procedures** on lusail.com — confirming it is a
permit-gating requirement. ⚠️ **It is dated 2014 and cites an older GSAS edition — verify currency
with LCAC before relying on its numeric parameters.**

Also confirmed: *"100% of FIFA World Cup Qatar 2022™ stadiums are GSAS Certified"*, and GORD's
chairman reports *"Over 2,400 projects in Qatar are GSAS certified."*

## Design Consequence

Where GSAS applies, **appoint the consultant before concept design is fixed.** Several categories —
Urban Connectivity, Site, Energy, Indoor Environment — are decided by orientation, massing, shading
and envelope, and are expensive to recover once the massing is agreed.

Note the interaction with [[KAHRAMAA Energy and Water Conservation Code]]: the KAHRAMAA envelope
U-values and SHGC limits are a **mandatory national floor** enforced through the electricity
connection, while GSAS Energy is a **scored** system. Meeting KAHRAMAA does not by itself earn GSAS
points; exceeding it does.
