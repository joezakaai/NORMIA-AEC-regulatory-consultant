# QIN Villa Type B — Openings and Privacy

> [!info] [[NORMIA]] · [[Authority Directory]] · [[Qatar]] · Discipline: [[Planning]]
> Source: `Qetaifan Island North District — Planning & Design Guidelines, Residential & Mixed-Use Plots` — REV 2, May 2023, p.92, 97

## Requirement

**Design Guidelines Rev 2, p.92 — OPENINGS:**

> **Side setback:** openings (e.g. windows, balconies and terraces) are allowed in walls from
> 3.0m setback but **recommended** to be screened for visual privacy from neighboring
> properties.
>
> **Front setback:** openings in front elevations must be setback min 5.0m and do not have to
> be screened.
>
> **Rear setback:** openings are allowed in GF rear and above levels. Openings on all levels
> between 6.0m and 10.0m setback do not have to be screened.
>
> **Balconies & terraces:** all balconies & terraces open to sky whose sides face neighboring
> plots **must have 1.8m high solid screen parapet** for privacy.

**Design Guidelines Rev 2, p.97 (Sheet 6/7):**

> Elevations that include openings (windows, balconies & terraces) are to adhere to 3m min.
> setback from the plot boundary. Windows may additionally be **angled or recessed** for
> additional privacy.

## Value / Criterion

| Condition | Requirement | Screening | Classification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Side elevation openings | From 3.0 m setback | **Recommended** | BEST PRACTICE |
| Front elevation openings | Min 5.0 m setback | Not required | MANDATORY |
| Rear elevation openings | GF and above | Not required between 6.0 m and 10.0 m setback | MANDATORY |
| Balcony/terrace open to sky, side facing neighbour | **1.8 m high solid screen parapet** | **Mandatory** | MANDATORY |

## Design Consequence

Privacy is handled as a hard geometric rule rather than an aesthetic preference, and the
mandatory item is the **1.8 m solid screen parapet** on any open-to-sky balcony or terrace
whose side faces a neighbouring plot. At 1.8 m that is above eye level and will read as a
wall in elevation — design it as architecture from the outset. It cannot be
value-engineered into a glass balustrade.

Side windows are permitted from the 3 m setback with screening only *recommended*, so there
is real latitude. The DG's own suggestion — angling or recessing windows — is the practical
answer where neighbours sit on both flanks.

Note the asymmetry: front openings need 5 m and no screening; rear openings are free above
6 m. **The side elevations are where the design tension sits.**

## Interpretation Notes

The word "recommended" places side-opening screening in the **BEST PRACTICE** category under
the DG's own definition (§2.1, p.38: "Guidelines" is a suggested preference, "Controls" are
mandatory rules). The 1.8 m parapet is written as "must have" and is therefore MANDATORY.

This distinction matters at DC-1: unscreened side windows are defensible; an unscreened
side-facing roof terrace is not.
