# QIN Villa Type B — Setbacks

> [!info] [[NORMIA]] · [[Authority Directory]] · [[Qatar]] · Discipline: [[Planning]]
> Source: `Qetaifan Island North District — Planning & Design Guidelines, Residential & Mixed-Use Plots` — REV 2, May 2023, p.92, 93

## Requirement

**Design Guidelines Rev 2, p.92 — Min. Setbacks Criteria:**

> **5m from Access road** / **3m from Neighbor sides** / **6m from Rear Side** /
> **1.50 architectural elements only from neighbor side / no openings allowed**
>
> Swimming pool **minimum 2m from external plot boundary**.

**Design Guidelines Rev 2, p.93 — Key Parameter F:**

> **Front Setback: min setback 5m and max setback 10m for main villa building.**
> This is to maintain a cohesive street alignment.

**Design Guidelines Rev 2, p.93 — Key Parameter H:**

> Cantilevered projections such as balconies **should remain within setback limits**. Only
> architectural elements such as decorations, shades, canopies etc. can project 1.5m from
> neighbor side.

**Design Guidelines Rev 2, p.92 — Ancillary Buildings:**

> Car shade allowed within neighbor side setback with **max. depth 6.00m**.

## Value / Criterion

| Parameter | Value | Unit | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Front setback (access road) — minimum | 5.00 | m | |
| Front setback — **maximum** | 10.00 | m | Main villa building; street alignment control |
| Side setback (neighbour sides) | 3.00 | m | |
| Rear setback | 6.00 | m | |
| Architectural element projection, neighbour side | 1.50 | m | **No openings permitted** |
| Swimming pool from external plot boundary | 2.00 | m | minimum |
| Car shade depth in neighbour side setback | 6.00 | m | maximum |

## Design Consequence

The control most often missed is the **maximum 10 m front setback**. The villa may not be
pushed deep into the plot to create a large forecourt — QP is enforcing a cohesive street
alignment across the precinct. The main villa's front face must therefore sit in a **5 m
band**, between 5 m and 10 m from the front plot boundary. Fix that line first; it drives
the whole plan.

The 1.5 m neighbour-side projection is for *architectural elements only* — decorations,
shades, canopies — and they may carry **no openings**. A cantilevered balcony is not an
architectural element for this purpose and must stay wholly within the setback lines.

## Interpretation Notes

The maximum front setback appears in the "KEY PARAMETERS" panel on p.93, not in the
"EXISTING REGULATIONS SUMMARY" or "ADDITIONAL REGULATIONS" panels on p.92, and it does not
appear on Plot Building Regulation Sheets. Under the DG's own Guidelines/Controls
distinction (§2.1, p.38) it reads as a Control, but its absence from the legal-basis
document makes explicit confirmation at pre-application worthwhile.
