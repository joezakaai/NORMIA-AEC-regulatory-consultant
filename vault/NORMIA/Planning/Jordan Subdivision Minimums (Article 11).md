# Jordan Subdivision Minimums (Article 11)

> [!info] [[NORMIA]] · [[Jordan]] · [[Planning]] · [[Jordan Building Regulation 2022]]
> **المادة 11-أ — لغايات إفراز الأراضي وتقسيمها تطبق الأحكام التنظيمية التالية**
> "For the purposes of land subdivision and partition, the following planning provisions apply."

## Design Consequence

These are the **entry tickets**. A plot below the minimum area or frontage for its class cannot be
created by subdivision, which makes them the first test in any Jordanian feasibility study — before
coverage, height or FAR are even considered.

Two points that catch teams out:

**Frontage is an independent test, not a derived one.** A 1,000 m² plot in سكن أ with only 20 m of
frontage fails, even though the area is satisfied. On irregular or corner plots, check frontage
first.

**The jump from سكن ج to سكن ب to سكن أ is large.** Minimum area doubles from 500 m² to 1,000 m²
across two classes while minimum frontage rises only from 18 m to 25 m — so higher-class zones
produce deeper plots, which interacts directly with the larger rear setbacks in
[[Jordan Development Control Table (Article 16)]].

## Interpretation Notes

Article 11(b): for planning uses **not expressly provided for** in this regulation, the planning
provisions under which the zoning plan was ratified apply.

Article 11(c): for buildings proposed **above existing buildings** licensed before this regulation
came into force, the planning provisions under which the existing building was licensed apply.
Where an existing building already encroaches on a setback, encroachment fees are **not** collected
for the proposed addition if those encroachments no longer conflict with this regulation.
