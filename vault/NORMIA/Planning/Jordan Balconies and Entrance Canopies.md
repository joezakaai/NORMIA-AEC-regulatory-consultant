# Jordan Balconies and Entrance Canopies

> [!info] [[NORMIA]] · [[Jordan]] · [[Planning]] · [[Jordan Development Control Table (Article 16)]]

## Balconies in the front setback — الشرفات ضمن الارتداد الأمامي

For proposed residential buildings in residential classes (أ، ب، ج، د), **on roads not less than
12 m wide**:

| Residential class | Maximum balcony depth within the setback |
| --- | --- |
| **سكن أ** | **1.50 m** |
| **سكن ب** | **1.25 m** |
| **سكن ج** | **1.25 m** |
| **سكن د** | **1.00 m** |

Further conditions:

1. A balcony depth **inside the building line** of not less than **50%** of the balcony's depth in
   the setback must be provided.
2. The balcony **width must not exceed 50%** of the building frontage.
3. The balcony area in the front setback is **excluded from the coverage percentage**, up to a
   maximum of **5%** of the building area permitted under this regulation.

> The owner must lodge a **notarised guarantee of five thousand dinars per balcony** undertaking not
> to enclose it or alter its features. If the balcony is used other than for its intended purpose,
> or is enclosed, the guarantee is **forfeited** and encroachment fees are collected.

## Entrance canopy — مظلة المدخل

The roof covering part of the setback in residential zones, for protection from weather:

| Location | Conditions |
| --- | --- |
| **Front setback** | Width ≤ **150%** of the stair core width or the front building entrance, **maximum 4 m**; a front setback of not less than **half** the prescribed planning setback must remain |
| **Side or rear setback** | Width ≤ the stair core width, **maximum 3 m**; depth ≤ **1.5 m** or **50%** of the side or rear setback, **whichever is less**; must **not rest on columns or walls** |

Both cases:

- The canopy roof must be at a **level different** from the roof level of the floor it adjoins.
- **Its roof must not be used for any purpose whatsoever.**

## Design Consequence

**Balcony depth is the one control that varies by residential class**, and it rewards the higher
classes: 1.50 m in سكن أ against 1.00 m in سكن د. On a narrow سكن د frontage the balcony is barely
usable at a metre, which pushes façade design toward recessed loggias inside the building line
instead.

**The 50% inside-the-line rule is the real constraint.** A 1.50 m balcony in the setback obliges at
least 0.75 m of balcony *inside* the building line — so the total balcony depth is 2.25 m and the
internal plan has to give up that depth. It is not a free projection.

**The five-thousand-dinar guarantee per balcony is a commercial fact, not just a legal one.** On a
building with twelve balconies that is sixty thousand dinars tied up. Raise it with the client at
concept stage, and design balconies that will not tempt later enclosure.

**The side/rear canopy may not rest on columns or walls** — it must cantilever. That is a
structural decision that has to be made when the canopy is first drawn.
