# QIN Villa Type B — Ancillary Buildings

> [!info] [[NORMIA]] · [[Authority Directory]] · [[Qatar]] · Discipline: [[Planning]]
> Source: `Qetaifan Island North District — Planning & Design Guidelines, Residential & Mixed-Use Plots` — REV 2, May 2023, p.92, 98

## Requirement

**Design Guidelines Rev 2, p.92 — Existing Regulations Summary:**

> All Ancillary buildings (except Driver or Guard room): **2m from the internal face of front
> boundary wall** and can locate at side boundary walls without opening. **Ancillary buildings
> are not permitted at rear setback.**

**Design Guidelines Rev 2, p.92 — ANCILLARY BUILDINGS:**

> Refuse Alcove, Driver, Majlis or Guard building: can locate on front of boundary wall and
> side boundary walls; while **no openings on the road side are allowed**.
>
> Other Ancillary Buildings: must be with setback minimum of **2m away from the villa** and
> might locate on rear boundary wall of villa. **Ancillary buildings are not permitted to
> attach at rear boundary.**
>
> Ancillary Structure length: Maximum cumulative length of all ancillary buildings must be
> **less than 50% of front road side or 75% of neighbor side wall length**.
>
> **Basements of any type not allowed under any ancillary.**

**Design Guidelines Rev 2, p.98 (Sheet 7/7):**

> An ancillary building is a support building such as a **Majlis, an outside kitchen,
> servant's quarters, driver's room, guard room, storage, pool room** etc.
> **Ancillary buildings must be included in the overall GFA.**
> Ancillary buildings may abut side walls and maintain a **2 meters separation from the main
> villa**.

| Parameter | Value | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Cumulative length — front road side | < 50% of wall length | maximum |
| Cumulative length — neighbour side | < 75% of wall length | maximum |
| Ancillary structure height | 3.50 m to top of parapet | Boundary wall document |
| Openings on road side | Not permitted | Refuse alcove, driver, majlis, guard |
| Basement under ancillary | Not permitted | Any type |
| Counted in GFA | **Yes** | Explicit |

## Design Consequence

Ancillary buildings are where villa schemes in this precinct most often go wrong, for three
reasons.

**They consume the GFA cap.** Majlis, driver's room, guard room and store can absorb a large
share of the allowance before the main house is drawn. Schedule them first.

**They are length-limited along the walls.** Cumulative ancillary length must stay under 50%
of the front road side and 75% of the neighbour side wall length. A continuous service range
along a boundary is not available — the blocks must be broken up.

**The 2 m separation from the main villa is absolute**, and it interacts with fire safety:
the DG directs fire-hazard uses such as outside kitchens to Civil Defence requirements for
fire-resistant construction. See
[[QIN — Ancillary Fire Hazard Uses and Civil Defence]].

## Interpretation Notes

**The rear-boundary wording is internally contradictory within a single page.** p.92 states
that other ancillary buildings "might locate on rear boundary wall of villa" and, in the next
line, that they are "not permitted to attach at rear boundary" — while the Existing
Regulations Summary on the same page says ancillary buildings are "not permitted at rear
setback" at all.

NORMIA does not silently pick a reading. The conservative interpretation — **treat the rear
as unavailable** for ancillary structures — is recommended for concept design, with the
question put to CAC in writing before DC-1.
