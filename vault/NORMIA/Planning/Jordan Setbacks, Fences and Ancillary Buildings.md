# Jordan Setbacks, Fences and Ancillary Buildings

> [!info] [[NORMIA]] · [[Jordan]] · [[Planning]] · [[Jordan Development Control Table (Article 16)]]

## 1. Fences — الأسوار

| Element | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Fence height above natural ground, **front** | **1.5 m** maximum |
| Fence height above natural ground, **rear and sides** | **2 m** maximum |
| Fill (الطمم) level in setbacks | not more than **1 m** above natural ground level at its location |

Fill is permitted **beneath each uncovered car parking space in the side setback** in residential
zones, and at the **front boundary** of the plot, so as to become level with the street entrance.

The committee may **exempt** ministry, government department and public institution buildings,
diplomatic mission buildings, schools, and special-use buildings from these fence height limits.

## 2. Roofing over setbacks — تسقيف الارتدادات

The committee may permit roofing over setbacks, **each case studied on its own merits**, based on a
**detailed topographic plan** attached to the application, provided the roof supports the street,
the retaining wall or a rocky face, and the roof level is **below natural ground level on all
sides**.

**Roofing over the front setback on the street side** is permitted where the roof supports the
street, the retaining wall or a rocky face, subject to:

| Condition | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Roof length | must not exceed the length of the building frontage on the street |
| Roof level | must match the level of the adjoining street; **in all cases must not exceed 45 cm** |
| Multiple streets | roof to be on the upper or lower street side, or both |

**Use beneath roofs in setbacks is restricted** to general building services: car parks, vehicle
passages, water wells, water, diesel and gas tanks, central heating and cooling rooms, electricity
rooms and transformer stations, and **non-commercial** storage rooms.

Suspended **ramps and vehicle passages** are permitted in setbacks provided their floor level is
**below natural ground level** at that location.

> [!tip] A genuine allowance worth using
> **Roof areas in setbacks are excluded from both the total built area and the floor area.** Used
> well, this puts parking, tanks and plant into the setback zone at no cost to the countable
> envelope — but only where the topography supports it and the 45 cm level test is met.

## 3. Building service facilities — required of the owner

1. **Water collection wells.** Discharging them to the **sanitary sewer network, decomposition
   tanks, absorption pits or neighbouring land is prohibited**. Built to conditions and
   specifications set by the committee.
2. **Decomposition, drainage or collection tanks and collection pits** are to be designed where no
   sewer network is available.

## 4. Ancillary buildings — الأبنية الفرعية (Article 34)

### (a) In all residential zones

| Condition | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Area ratio | **≤ 5%** of plot area, **additional to** the permitted coverage |
| Absolute cap | **≤ 25 m²** in any case |
| Front setback | per the zoning provisions of the area |
| External height | **≤ 2.6 m** above natural ground level |
| Use | limited to **serving the main building**. Must **not** be a boiler room, electricity generator room, or any use causing nuisance to neighbours |
| Roof | no opening in its roof leading to that roof; the roof **must not be used for any purpose** |
| Attachment | must **not be attached** to the existing building on the plot |

### (b) In industrial zones

Ancillary buildings are permitted **in the side setbacks**, area **≤ 5%** of plot area and **≤ 100 m²**,
used for **guarding purposes**, provided a front setback of **not less than 5 m** is maintained.

## Design Consequence

**The residential ancillary allowance is additive, not deducted** — 5% of plot area up to 25 m²
sits *on top of* the coverage in [[Jordan Development Control Table (Article 16)]]. On a 1,000 m²
سكن أ plot that is a free 25 m² beyond the 39%. It is small, but it is genuinely extra, and the
2.6 m height cap keeps it single-storey and subordinate.

**Detachment is mandatory.** The ancillary building may not touch the main building, which rules
out the common move of a guard room or store bolted onto the villa. Plan the separation early.

**Fence heights are asymmetric** — 1.5 m front against 2 m sides and rear. On a sloping site the
1 m fill cap in setbacks interacts with this and with the height datum in
[[Jordan Building Height Measurement (Article 15)]]; resolve levels, fill and fence together rather
than in sequence.
