# Lebanon — Safety, Public Health and Architectural Servitudes
### المادة العاشرة — إرتفاقات خاصة بالسلامة والصحة العامة والمنظورات المعمارية · decree pp. 43–47

Parent: [[Lebanon Building Law Implementing Decree 15874-2005]]

This article carries the public-health and amenity servitudes that sit alongside the geometric controls: light wells above roofs, the **minimum façade width**, and the treatment of plant on balconies.

## Minimum façade width (عرض الواجهة الأدنى)

The decree fixes a minimum façade width for the parts of a building that carry habitable rooms. Figures 47, 48 and 49 give three configurations; the governing dimension shown on each is **> 5 m**.

## Cooling units on balconies

Figure 50 sets the conditions for placing condensers and cooling plant on balconies — they must sit behind **حواجب** (screens) and must not obstruct the **تهوئة طبيعية** (natural ventilation) of the space.
