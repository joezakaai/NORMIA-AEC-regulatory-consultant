# QIN Villa Type B — Roof Terraces

> [!info] [[NORMIA]] · [[Authority Directory]] · [[Qatar]] · Discipline: [[Planning]]
> Source: `Qetaifan Island North District — Planning & Design Guidelines, Residential & Mixed-Use Plots` — REV 2, May 2023, p.97

## Requirement

**Design Guidelines Rev 2, p.97 (Sheet 6/7):**

> **A** — No roof terrace is permitted in this area due to inadequate screening. Access to
> this roof space should be restricted.
> **B** — Roof terrace is permitted here as this elevation has adequate screening from
> neighboring plots.
> **C** — No screening to windows or terraces is required on front and rear elevations
> **for beachfront and waterfront villas**.
> **D** — Roof terrace is permitted here as it is **at least 6m away from side and rear plot
> boundaries**.
> **E** — Side openings on all levels at 3.0m setback recommended to be screened.
>
> Roof areas created through the articulation of the building massing **can only be accessible
> if appropriate screening requirements are in place**. If screening requirements do not apply,
> access should be restricted by **prohibiting patio doors or full height windows** from
> opening onto these spaces. **Non accessible roofs should be designed with materials and
> finishes that discourage their habitable use.**

## Value / Criterion

| Condition | Roof terrace permitted? |
| --- | --- |
| At least 6 m from side **and** rear plot boundaries | Yes |
| Elevation with adequate screening from neighbouring plots | Yes |
| Area with inadequate screening | **No** — access must be restricted |
| Front/rear elevations — **beachfront and waterfront villas only** | Yes, no screening required |

## Design Consequence

The operative threshold is **6 m from side and rear plot boundaries** — double the 3 m
building setback. A roof terrace cannot simply occupy the roof of the floor below; it must
be inset a further 3 m on each constrained side.

Where a roof area is not eligible, the DG requires positive design action rather than an
absence of furniture: **no patio doors or full-height windows** may open onto it, and the
surface must be finished to discourage habitable use. Reviewers check this at DC-2. A flat
roof with a threshold-level door and a paved finish reads as an undeclared terrace whatever
the drawing labels it.

## Interpretation Notes

**Item C applies only to beachfront and waterfront villas.** Villa Type B is the *Parkside*
typology, so item C does **not** apply to Type B plots — front and rear terraces on a
Parkside villa are not automatically exempt from screening.

Flagged because this page carries guidance for several typologies at once and is commonly
circulated in isolation, which invites the misreading.
