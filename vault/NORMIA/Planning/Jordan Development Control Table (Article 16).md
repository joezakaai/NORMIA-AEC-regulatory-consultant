# Jordan Development Control Table (Article 16)

> [!info] [[NORMIA]] · [[Jordan]] · [[Planning]] · [[Jordan Building Regulation 2022]]
> **المادة 16-أ — مع مراعاة أي أحكام خاصة ترد على المخططات الهيكلية أو التفصيلية تضع قيوداً أو
> تمنح تسهيلات يسمح بإقامة الأبنية وفقاً للأحكام التالية**
> "Subject to any special provisions in structural or detailed plans imposing restrictions or
> granting facilities, buildings may be erected in accordance with the following provisions."

**This is the core development control instrument for Jordan.** All values read visually from the
gazette scan, pages 15–16.

> [!warning] Structural and detailed plans override
> Article 16(a) opens by subordinating itself to special provisions on the **structural or detailed
> plan** for the area. Always obtain the local zoning decision (أحكام التنظيم) for the plot before
> applying this table — it may restrict or relax these figures.

## Article 16(b) — High buildings (الأبنية العالية)

The competent planning committee may permit high buildings under these provisions:

| Zone | Front ≥ | Side ≥ | Rear ≥ | Coverage ≤ | Floors ≤ | Height ≤ | Green ≥ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **السكني** Residential | 8 m | 10 m | 10 m | 35% | **8** | **30 m** | 20% |
| **التجاري** (مركزي، معارض، طولي، محلي) Commercial | 8 m | 10 m | 10 m | 50% | **8** | **35 m** | 20% |
| **المتعدد الاستعمال** Mixed use | 15 m | 12 m | 12 m | 50% | **16** | **50 m** | 15% |

> Note the trade: high buildings buy height and storeys at the cost of **much larger side and rear
> setbacks** (10–12 m instead of 2.5–6 m) and a **lower coverage** in residential (35% against
> 39–55%). It is a tower-in-open-space bargain, not a free uplift.

## Article 16(c) — Height increases

| Trigger | Bonus |
| --- | --- |
| Every **100 m²** above the minimum prescribed subdivision area | **+35 cm** of height |
| Every **1 m** increase over the minimum required **side and front** setbacks | **+30 cm** of height |

## Article 16(d) — Absolute cap

> "لا يسمح بأن يزيد ارتفاع البناء على (60) متراً."
> **Building height may not exceed 60 metres**, notwithstanding anything else in the Article.

## Article 16(e) — Exclusions

Areas regulated under **green, rural or agricultural residential** provisions, areas with **special
provisions**, and **plots served by roads narrower than 20 m** are excluded from paragraphs (b),
(c) and (d).

## Article 16(f) — Additional floor

The committee may license **one additional floor of up to 4 m** for buildings proposed on plots
fronting streets **not less than 14 m** wide, subject to providing the parking required for that
floor. **Not permitted** on plots whose zoning plan was ratified **before 16/1/2018**.

## Article 16(g) — Coverage relief

The Council may approve buildings **without complying with the coverage percentage** in the table
above, provided plans for the entire proposed building are submitted and the prescribed **floor
area ratio (النسبة الطابقية) and setbacks** are complied with.

## Design Consequence

**Coverage rises as the zone class falls, while setbacks shrink.** سكن أ gives 39% on a 1,000 m²
minimum plot; سكن د gives 55% on 250 m². So the small-plot classes are the dense ones, and the
constraint that bites shifts from setbacks (in أ) to coverage and parking (in د).

**Four floors and 17 m is the residential norm across all four classes.** Height does not vary by
class — only footprint does. That means the route to more area in a residential zone is the
Article 16(c) bonuses, the Article 16(f) additional floor, or the Article 16(b) high-building
regime, not the zone class.

**The 20 m road test in Article 16(e) is decisive and easy to miss.** A plot served by a road under
20 m wide cannot use the high-building table, the height bonuses, or the 60 m cap provisions at
all. Establish road width before promising a client any uplift.

**Ribbon commercial is the densest ordinary zone at 70% coverage**, with the side setback waived
for the first 14 m of depth — designed for continuous street frontage.

## Interpretation Notes

The table gives **coverage (النسبة المئوية)**, not floor area ratio. **FAR (النسبة الطابقية) is
referenced in Article 16(g) but is not tabulated in this Article** — it is set by the local zoning
decision for the area. Do not assume a FAR from the coverage and floor count; obtain it.

"2 + roof" (2 وروف) for green/rural and agricultural residential means two floors plus a roof
floor, with the 9 m height limit measured **excluding** the roof floor — a genuine allowance, not a
rounding.
