# Lebanon — Parking (المرآب)
### المادتان الخامسة عشرة والسادسة عشرة · decree pp. 77–99

Parent: [[Lebanon Building Law Implementing Decree 15874-2005]]

> [!important] Definition — and it is binding on every building
> «يجب أن تتضمن تصاميم الأبنية من أي نوع كانت ومهما كانت وجهة استعمالها مرآباً أو أكثر.»
> The garage is «**مكان مخصص لوقوف السيارات مكشوفاً كان أم مسقوفاً، مقفلاً أم لا، واقعاً بمستوى الأرض أم تحته أم فوقه، يسهل ولوج السيارات إليه وخروجها منه وسـيرها بداخله**» — open or roofed, enclosed or not, at, below or above ground, which cars can easily enter, leave and move within.
> The garage belonging to each part of the building is **an indivisible part of that unit** (جزء لا يتجزأ) when it is let, subdivided or sold.

## 1 · Required number of spaces — أولاً/أ (new buildings)

| # | Use class | Required ratio | Min / Max |
|---|---|---|---|

| ٢ | Furnished apartments/rooms for tourists and students | **1 per 2 apartments** | each apartment **≤ 50 m²** |
| ٣-١ | Hotels **in governorate and caza centres** | **1 per 4 rooms** | + restaurants/cafés/night clubs on the same plot |
| ٣-٢ | Hotels **elsewhere** | **1 per 2 rooms** | as above |
| ٤ | Motel | **1 per unit** (room or apartment) | — |

> [!note] Areas EXCLUDED from the computation base *(amended by Decree 617/2007, Art. 1 — رابع وعشرون)*
> External walls · balconies · verandas · loggias · decorative projections · sun-breakers · flower boxes · mezzanines not belonging to commercial shops whose clear height does not exceed **1.9 m** · stair and lift enclosures · tiled-roof attic structures serving the building · external passages connecting stairs and lifts to dwellings, offices or exploited areas.

**Change of use (أولاً/ب).** On a change of use or a change in the number of units, the garage under (أ) is imposed; where provision is impossible it may be replaced by the special fee, computed on **the difference** between the number of spaces before and after.

**Adding to a built plot (أولاً/ج).** The new drawings must show the parking that the existing permit provided, **plus** the parking required for the addition.
> **PROHIBITION:** «يمنع الترخيص بإضافة أية إنشاءات، من أعمدة أو غيرها، من شأنها أن تؤثر سلباً على المرآب المخصص للبناء الموجود في حال عدم تأمين مرآب بديل.»

### Special ramp-gradient cases — regime أ only

| Case | Permitted gradient |
|---|---|
| Normal ramp (> 15 m long) | **20%** max, transitions **≤ 10%** over ≥ 5 m |
| Ramp **≤ 15 m** long | **12%** continuous average, including transitions |
| **Demi rampes** in **demi-niveaux** garages | **12%** continuous average |
| Garage floor at **5%** gradient | **15%** continuous average for the ramp |

### Obstruction rules — ثانياً/٢

- **Regime أ:** each car must enter, park and leave without obstructing another. Obstruction is acceptable **only** within a private garage belonging to **one and the same ownership right**, and **not more than half** the cars belonging to that unit may be obstructed.
- **Regime ب:** in plots that cannot accommodate a **16 m × 16 m square** after the mandatory setbacks, one car may obstruct **another car of a different ownership right** in the basement and upper floors, and **one or two cars** on the ground floor.

## 3 · Article 16 — Additional and collective (public) garages · pp. 96–99

- **المرآب الإضافي** — additional garage beyond the required count.
- **المرآب الجماعي للعموم** — collective public garage.
- **Incentives (ثالثاً):** occupation of public property to build public garages; and **an increase in the exploitation factor where an additional garage is provided and assigned to the municipality** *(amended by Decree 617)*.
- **Sale and lease** of additional spaces is governed by رابعاً (p.99) *(amended by Decree 617)*.
