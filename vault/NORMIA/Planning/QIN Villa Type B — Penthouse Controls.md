# QIN Villa Type B — Penthouse Controls

> [!info] [[NORMIA]] · [[Authority Directory]] · [[Qatar]] · Discipline: [[Planning]]
> Source: `Qetaifan Island North District — Planning & Design Guidelines, Residential & Mixed-Use Plots` — REV 2, May 2023, p.92, 93, 94

## Requirement

**Design Guidelines Rev 2, p.92:**

> Penthouse Coverage — **40% of first floor footprint** with a minimum **3m from first-floor
> front and rear façade**

**Design Guidelines Rev 2, p.93 — Key Parameter G:**

> Penthouse position: penthouses must be within a minimum 3.0m from the first-floor front and
> rear façade or windows on the side. **Penthouse sides with windows should be min. 6 m from
> boundary wall**, except for small windows for the purpose of ventilating bathrooms.

**Design Guidelines Rev 2, p.92 — Openings:**

> Penthouse openings: on facades facing neighbors, **openings are not allowed in Penthouse
> unless it's minimum 6.0m from boundary wall** except for small windows for the purpose of
> ventilating bathrooms.

## Value / Criterion

| Parameter | Value | Unit | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Penthouse coverage | 40 | % of first-floor footprint | |
| Setback from first-floor front façade | 3.00 | m | minimum |
| Setback from first-floor rear façade | 3.00 | m | minimum |
| Penthouse side with openings — from boundary wall | 6.00 | m | minimum; bathroom ventilation windows exempt |

## Design Consequence

The penthouse is a constrained third level, not a free floor. Capped at 40% of the
first-floor footprint **and** stepped back 3 m from both the front and rear first-floor
façades, the usable penthouse on a typical plan is a narrow central band.

The 6 m rule is the one that catches design teams. Any penthouse elevation **facing a
neighbour** may not have openings unless that elevation is at least 6 m from the boundary
wall. Since the side setback is only 3 m, a penthouse aligned with the floor below will be
**blind on both flanks**. Delivering side views means pulling the penthouse in to 6 m off
the boundary — which usually costs more area than the 40% cap does.

Only small bathroom ventilation windows escape the rule. Decide early whether the client
values side outlook enough to pay for it in area.
