# Lebanon — View Distance and Daylight (مدى وقوع النظر)
### المادة التاسعة · decree pp. 37–42

Parent: [[Lebanon Building Law Implementing Decree 15874-2005]] · See also [[Lebanon Building Envelope (Article 7)]]

> [!important] Core obligation
> Every room intended for habitation or occupation must be secured a **مدى وقوع نظر** — a view distance — onto the open air. It is the Lebanese equivalent of a daylight-and-outlook right, and it drives both room geometry and the court envelope of Article 7.

## 1 · When an opening serves a room — أولاً/١

| Test | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Depth** | **P ≤ 5 × H** — room depth measured from the **outer edge of the opening** inward at the opening axis, against the room's **clear height under the ceiling** |
| Through a veranda, loggia, balcony or cornice | depth is counted from the **outer edge of that element**, excluding the tile-roof projection |
| Parts of a room at different clear heights | use the **minimum** clear height |
| Sloping ceiling | use the **average** clear height — H = (H1+H2)/2 |
| Persistence | the 5× rule keeps applying **as long as no other opening secures a legal view distance and envelope** |

| Room-dimension limits | Rule |
|---|---|
| Room length against opening width | **L ≤ 11 × a** |
| Opening area against room area | **f ≥ S / 10** |
| Minimum view distance | **E ≥ 4.50 m** |
| Legal court | **≥ 30 m²** containing a **4.50 × 5.50 m** rectangle |

## 2 · Width of the view field — أولاً/٢

The view field (**حقل وقوع النظر**) must be at least **2 m** wide, and is measured on the axis of the opening.

## 3 · Spaces that do NOT require a view distance — ثانياً

Kitchens under a stated size, bathrooms, WCs, corridors, stores and similar service spaces are excluded — but they still require **ventilation** under ثالثاً.

## 4 · Ventilation of exempt spaces — ثالثاً

| Case | Provision |
|---|---|
| أ | small kitchens, bathrooms and WCs |
| ب | special-use buildings |
| ج | fuel tanks, boilers and generators in basements |
| د | unclassified warehouses and garages in basements — table in the decree |
| هـ | residual cases |

### ★ Light well (المنور) heights above roofs — p. 42

> «يجب أن يتراوح علو المنور فوق سقف السفلي الأول بين المتر والمترين بما فيه وسائل الحماية ضد الأمطار وأن لا يقل عن المتر ونصف في الطابق الأخير المكشوف على الهواء.»

| Symbol | Rule |
|---|---|
| **e1** — light-well height above the first-basement roof | **1 m ≤ e1 ≤ 2 m**, including rain protection |
| **b1** — width serving basement ventilation | **≥ 0.30 m** |
| **e2** — height above the last roof | **≥ 1.50 m** |
