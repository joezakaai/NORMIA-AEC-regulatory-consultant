# QIN Villa Type B — Basement and GFA Exclusion

> [!info] [[NORMIA]] · [[Authority Directory]] · [[Qatar]] · Discipline: [[Planning]]
> Source: `Qetaifan Island North District — Planning & Design Guidelines, Residential & Mixed-Use Plots` — REV 2, May 2023, p.92, 94, 95

## Requirement

**Design Guidelines Rev 2, p.92:**

> Basement — **One level of basement is permitted following building setback.** Deviations
> may be considered on a case-by-case basis.

**Design Guidelines Rev 2, p.94 — Key Parameter F (Sections A-A and B-B):**

> Basement: max 1 level is permitted following building setback. **Area is excluded from GFA
> only if both naturally ventilated and non-habitable. Habitable spaces cannot collectively
> exceed a maximum 50% of basement footprint.**

**Design Guidelines Rev 2, p.95 (Sheet 4/7):**

> Non-habitable spaces in basement are excluded from GFA.

**Design Guidelines Rev 2, p.92 — Ancillary Buildings:**

> **Basements of any type not allowed under any ancillary.**

## Value / Criterion

| Parameter | Value | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basement levels permitted | 1 | Deviations case-by-case |
| Basement extent | Follows the **building setback** | Not the plot boundary |
| Habitable basement area | ≤ 50% of basement footprint | Collectively |
| GFA exclusion test | Naturally ventilated **AND** non-habitable | Both conditions required |
| Basement under ancillary buildings | Not permitted | Any type |

## Design Consequence

The basement is the most valuable piece of latitude in this typology, and the rule is
narrower than it looks. Area is excluded from GFA **only where it is both naturally
ventilated and non-habitable** — cumulative conditions, not alternatives. A mechanically
ventilated store is not excluded. A naturally ventilated home cinema is not excluded.

In practice: parking, plant, tanks and service space can go below ground without consuming
GFA. Habitable rooms cannot, and in any case may not exceed **50% of the basement
footprint**.

Two further constraints:

- The basement follows the **building setback**, not the plot boundary. There is no
  basement box extending under the garden to the boundary walls.
- **No basement of any type under any ancillary building** — no sunken majlis, no basement
  beneath the driver's block.

Natural ventilation in this envelope means light wells or a sunken court, which must
themselves sit within the building setback. Resolve that at concept stage; retrofitting a
light well after the plan is fixed usually fails the setback test.

## Interpretation Notes

The DG gives **no quantitative test** for "naturally ventilated" — no free-area percentage,
no openable-area ratio. The same basement could pass or fail on a reviewer's judgement.
Agree the test with CAC at pre-application before committing to a basement area strategy,
and do not assume a Qatar Construction Specifications default applies without written
agreement.
