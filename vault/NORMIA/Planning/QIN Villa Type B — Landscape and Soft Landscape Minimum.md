# QIN Villa Type B — Landscape and Soft Landscape Minimum

> [!info] [[NORMIA]] · [[Authority Directory]] · [[Qatar]] · Discipline: [[Planning]]
> Source: `Qetaifan Island North District — Planning & Design Guidelines, Residential & Mixed-Use Plots` — REV 2, May 2023, p.92

## Requirement

**Design Guidelines Rev 2, p.92 — LANDSCAPE:**

> Front gardens to incorporate **at least 2 mature trees** in line with landscape guidelines
> and controls as a contribution to streetscape character. **Min. 5% of the plot to be soft
> landscape with irrigated vegetation.**

## Value / Criterion

| Parameter | Value | Unit | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Soft landscape with irrigated vegetation | 5 | % of plot, minimum | |
| Mature trees in front garden | 2 | trees, minimum | |

## Design Consequence

A low bar numerically, but it is a *soft, irrigated* requirement — gravel, decking and paving
do not count toward it. On a villa plot where half may be covered by building and much of the
remainder given to driveway, pool and terrace, genuinely planted irrigated ground still has
to be found and shown.

The two mature trees are a separate, additional obligation and are about streetscape
character, so expect them to be checked in elevation at DC-2, not only on the landscape plan.

Irrigation implies a source. Lusail's masterplan strategy is explicit about the creative reuse
of recycled water and low-demand xeriscape species — coordinate with Marafeq at Services
Review.

## Interpretation Notes

"Mature" is not quantified in the source — no caliper, height or container size. Agree the accepted definition with QP's landscape reviewer before specifying.
