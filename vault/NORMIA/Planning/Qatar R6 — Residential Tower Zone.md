# Qatar R6 — Residential Tower Zone

> [!info] [[NORMIA]] · [[Qatar]] · [[Planning]] · [[Qatar Zoning Code Register]] · [[QNMP Zoning Regulation Framework]]
> Zone code **R6** · Ministry of Municipality · Edition **12/04/2020**
> Source: https://www.mme.gov.qa/QatarMasterPlan/Downloads-qnmp/Regulation/English/01-Residential%20Zones/06-Residential%206%20-%20Eng%20-%2012042020.pdf

> [!warning] Applies to ordinary municipal land only
> Master-planned districts (Lusail, Qetaifan Island North, Msheireb, Education City, Qatar Free
> Zones) are outside this instrument — they are zoned **SD** and carry their own development
> control. See [[Qatar National GIS — QMap Source Assessment]] §4.

## Requirement

Lot-size-banded table. Quoted verbatim from the official PDF:

> FAR: "1.3" to "5.5" by lot size
> Building coverage: "50"
> Front setback: "3m"; side/rear: "1.5m to 6m"
> Height: "G + 10" for plots ≥1,620 m²
> Large-lot variation: "G+12 on a site area ≥4000m²"
> "At least one boundary of the lot shall have a minimum dimension of 20m"
> "Car parking is not permitted within the front setback."

## Design Consequence

Two controls here are absolute rather than banded, and both shape the scheme before any
massing is drawn:

**At least one lot boundary must be ≥ 20 m.** A long thin plot that fails this cannot support a
tower regardless of its area. Check it first.

**Car parking is prohibited in the front setback.** With only a 3 m front setback and a tower
programme, that pushes parking to basement or podium — a significant cost and buildability
decision that has to be made at feasibility, not later.

FAR up to 5.5 at G+10 (G+12 above 4,000 m²) makes R6 the densest residential zone, and plot
assembly above the 1,620 m² and 4,000 m² thresholds is where the value sits.
