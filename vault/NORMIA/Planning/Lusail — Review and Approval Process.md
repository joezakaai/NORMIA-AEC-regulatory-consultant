# Lusail — Review and Approval Process

> [!info] [[NORMIA]] · [[Authority Directory]] · [[Qatar]] · Discipline: [[Planning]]
> Source: `Qetaifan Island North District — Planning & Design Guidelines, Residential & Mixed-Use Plots` — REV 2, May 2023, p.31, 32, 33, 34, 35

## Requirement

**§1.6.3, p.31 — The Approving Authority:**

> **Lusail City Administration Complex (CAC), a Department within LREDC, will act as the
> development control authority** for Lusail City districts …
> **Qetaifan Projects (QP), the Master Developer of Qetaifan Island North District, will also
> act as the development control authority** in accordance with the Masterplan and with
> coordination with CAC.
>
> CAC shall be responsible for issuing **Concept Design, DC-1, Services Review and DC-2
> approvals**.
> **Al Daayen Municipality shall be responsible for the issuance of Building Permits** for
> developments which have received approval from CAC.
> CAC shall have the power to **interpret these Design Guidelines and Controls and to clarify
> any ambiguities** … **CAC's decision shall be final and binding on the Developer.**

**§1.6.4, pp.32–35 — the nine steps:**

> Step 1 Project Initiation · Step 2 Pre-Application Meeting · Step 3 Concept Design Review &
> Approval · Step 4 DC1 · Step 5 Building Permit (optional) · Step 6 Services Review ·
> Step 7 DC2 · Step 8 Building Permit · Step 9 Certificate of Completion

**Step 1:** "The Plot owner must initiate a project with LREDC and QP by submitting a letter to
the QP, **appointing a qualified local registered consultant** to act on his behalf."

**Step 4 (DC-1):** "**Initial fire safety approvals are also required at this stage from Qatar
Civil Defense Department** as well as **Electrical and Water supply approval from KM
department**."

**Step 6 (Services Review):** "**All authorizations shall be obtained from relevant agencies
prior to DC-2 submittal.**" 

## Value / Criterion

| Step | Stage | Approving body |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Project Initiation — appoint a **qualified local registered consultant** | LREDC + QP |
| 2 | Pre-Application Meeting (optional) | QP + CAC |
| 3 | Concept Design Review & Approval | **QP** Architectural Review Committee |
| 4 | **DC-1** Design Control 1 — *plus QCDD fire and KAHRAMAA electrical/water approvals* | QP + CAC |
| 5 | Building Permit (optional, after DC-1) | Al Daayen Municipality via CAC |
| 6 | Services Review — Ooredoo, Marafeq, water/irrigation, drainage, MoI/QCDD | Relevant agencies |
| 7 | **DC-2** Design Control 2 | CAC |
| 8 | Building Permit (if not taken after DC-1) | Al Daayen Municipality via CAC |
| 9 | Certificate of Completion | CAC |

## Design Consequence

This is a **master-developer-led** process: the municipal building permit sits *inside* it, not
before it. Al Daayen Municipality issues the permit only on the basis of a CAC approval.

Three programme consequences worth building the design programme around:

- **A qualified local registered consultant must be appointed at Step 1.** A gating
  requirement, not a formality.
- **QCDD fire approval and KAHRAMAA electrical/water approval land at DC-1**, alongside the
  first architectural approval — far earlier than teams usually plan. Fire strategy and
  utility loads must be resolved at schematic stage, with submissions running in parallel with
  the QP/CAC architectural review rather than after it.
- **Every utility authorisation must precede DC-2.** Services Review is a hard gate.

CAC holds interpretive power and its decision is **final and binding** — so ambiguities in the
Design Guidelines are resolved by asking CAC, and that answer, once given, is the applicable
rule.

## Interpretation Notes

**Internal inconsistency in the source.** The flow diagram on p.35 labels both Step 4 and
Step 5 as "DC1 — DESIGN CONTROL 1 REVIEW & APPROVAL (by QP & CAC)", whereas the narrative on
pp.32 and 34 gives Step 5 as "Building Permit (optional)". The narrative is internally
consistent across two pages and is the better reading; the diagram appears to carry a
copy-paste error. Flagged so the diagram is not cited as authority.

The DG also states pre-application is not required for "small proposals such as single villa
applications". Given the unresolved boundary wall conflict and several undefined technical
tests in this document set, requesting one anyway is prudent.
