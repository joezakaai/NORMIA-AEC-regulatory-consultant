# QIN Villa Type B — Height, Floors and Level Controls

> [!info] [[NORMIA]] · [[Authority Directory]] · [[Qatar]] · Discipline: [[Planning]]
> Source: `Qetaifan Island North District — Planning & Design Guidelines, Residential & Mixed-Use Plots` — REV 2, May 2023, p.92, 93, 95

## Requirement

**Design Guidelines Rev 2, p.92:**

> Max. Number of Floors — **G + 1 + P (Penthouse)**
> Max. Heights — **15m to top of Penthouse roof top of parapet** / **3.5m for service block
> top of parapet** / **4.5m for majlis top of parapet** / Boundary wall: 3m on all sides

**Design Guidelines Rev 2, p.93 — Key Parameter B:**

> Maximum Building Height: measured **from the lowest side of the plot** to top of parapet
> and must not exceed **+15m including all parapets and roof structures**.

**Design Guidelines Rev 2, p.95 (Sheet 4/7) — level diagram:**

> Max. **+15m** — necessary screens to conceal roof structures / Penthouse
> Max. **+9m** — 1st Floor
> Max. **+5.5m** — Ground Floor
> Max. **+1m** Ground floor level above plot access level

## Design Consequence

Two details make this stricter than it first reads.

**The datum is the lowest side of the plot**, not the finished floor level and not the
average site level. On a sloping plot that can cost real height, so the surrounding road
and plot levels must be established before the section is fixed.

**The 15 m includes parapets and roof structures.** The DG's own level diagram places
"necessary screens to conceal roof structures" *under* the +15 m line — so rooftop plant
screening, lift overrun and pergolas all consume envelope. Allow for the screen height in
the section from the start rather than adding it after the massing is agreed.

The intermediate levels leave roughly 4 m floor-to-floor for ground and first. Comfortable,
but not enough for a double-height majlis volume plus a full penthouse without careful
section work.
