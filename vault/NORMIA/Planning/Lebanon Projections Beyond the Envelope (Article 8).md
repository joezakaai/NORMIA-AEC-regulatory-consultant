# Lebanon — Projections Beyond the Envelope (النتوء عن الغلاف)
### المادة الثامنة · decree pp. 33–36

Parent: [[Lebanon Building Law Implementing Decree 15874-2005]] · See also [[Lebanon Building Envelope (Article 7)]]

## 1 · Free, unenclosed balconies — أولاً/أ

| Street width **L** | Maximum projection **b** |
|---|---|
| **L < 9.00 m** | **0 m — any projection is prohibited** |
| **L ≥ 9.00 m** | **b ≤ 1.05 m** |

> «على الطريق التي تقل سعتها عن تسعة أمتار **يمنع أي نتوء**.»

## 2 · Ground-floor canopies (الرفارف) — أولاً/ب

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Street width | **L = X + 2R** (X = carriageway, R = setback each side) |
| Projection | **F = L / 8**, capped at **3.00 m** from the setback limit |
| Clearance from the curb | outer edge **≥ 0.30 m behind the curb (الشعيرة)** where a sidewalk exists |
| Status on subdivision | canopies are a **common part** (قسم مشترك) |
| Upper surface | may be used as flower boxes |

## 3 · Sun-breakers, cornices, flower boxes, decoration — أولاً/ج

- **≤ 0.60 m** beyond the street-side envelope line and beyond the building façade (**excluding** balconies, verandas and loggias).
- **Thermal insulation and damp-proofing** may project by **no more than the thickness of the works**, provided their height does not exceed **0.50 m above the roof of the last floor**.

## 4 · Minimum clear height under any projection

| Condition | Minimum |
|---|---|
| Above sidewalk level | **3.00 m** |
| Above street level where there is **no** sidewalk | **3.20 m** |

## 5 · Tile eaves, stair and lift cages, buttresses — أولاً/و

| Element | Street side | Court side |
|---|---|---|
| Stair / lift cages, tanks, parapets | **≤ 4.50 m** beyond the inclined envelope, buildings **> 15 m** | same conditions |
| Tile roof (ثكنة القرميد) | **1.05 m** vertical / **2.50 m** inclined | **2.50 m** inclined; vertical **1.05 m** if R ≥ 4.50 m, **0.60 m** if 1.50 ≤ R < 4.50 m, **0 m** if R < 1.50 m |
