# Lebanon — The Envelope (الغلاف)
### المادة السابعة · decree pp. 26–32

Parent: [[Lebanon Building Law Implementing Decree 15874-2005]]

> [!important] The envelope never beats the zone
> «مع عدم إمكانية تجاوز الارتفاع الأقصى المحدد في نظام المنطقة التي يقع فيها العقار، وفي حال عدم وجود أحكام خاصة تتعلق بالغلاف، يخضع إنشاء البناء للشروط التالية»
> Two constraints stack: the **zone's absolute maximum height**, which can never be exceeded, and the envelope geometry below.

## 1 · Envelope on streets — أولاً/أ

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Vertical line** | erected on the street / alignment boundary, **H = 2.5 × L** |
| **Absolute floor** | **≥ 15.00 m** regardless of street width |
| **Street width where setbacks are imposed** | **L = R1 + X + R2** — the distance between the two imposed setback lines *is* the street width |
| Where the vertical starts | sidewalk level (or street level if none), or the alignment's longitudinal profile, **taken at the middle of the façade** |
| **Inclined line** | from the top of the vertical, inward, at **2 : 5** (2 of base to 5 of height) |

**Sloping streets.** If the level difference between the two ends of the façade exceeds **3.50 m**, the façade is split into segments each with ≤ 3.50 m difference, and a vertical is taken at the middle of **each segment separately**.

**Street perpendicular to the façade** *(added by Decree 617/2007, Art. 1 — عاشراً)* — the street envelope applies **only** to the portions adjoining and facing the street.

## 2 · Envelope on a square — أولاً/ب

A plot fronting a square takes, for the verticals on that square **and on the streets branching off it to a depth of 30 m from the square's setback limit**, the width of **the widest branching street including its imposed setbacks**.

**Figure 23 — Plots on roads branching from a square benefit from the envelope on road L to a depth of 30 m**
*تستفيد واجهات العقارات على الطرق المتفرعة عن ساحة بعمق ٣٠م من الغلاف على الطريق*  · decree p.28

## 3 · Rear buildings — أولاً/ج

A rear building separated from the street building by an internal court may take the **street** envelope, provided:
- the separation between the two buildings is **≥ 4.50 m**, and
- the height of the buildings on the street is **≤ 5 × the separating distance** (the court width), within the envelope maxima.

## 4 · Corner of two or more streets — أولاً/د

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| L1 = R1 + X1 + R1' | full width of street 1 including setbacks |
| G1 = 2.5 × L1 | envelope height derived from street 1 |
| d ≤ 30 m | depth over which the wider street's envelope carries round the corner |

## 5 · Height on courts — ثانياً

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Vertical line** | on the building façade **excluding projections**, **H = 5 × E** |
| **E** | the **minimum** actual view distance secured from the rooms lit and ventilated onto that court |
| Where E is measured | between the vertical tangent to the façade at the **axis of the opening** and the vertical on the plot boundary — inside the view field, from the **most protruding point of the structure** facing the opening |
| Starting level | final ground level after levelling, at the **lowest point** where it meets the façade, **in each independent court**, disregarding the garage-ramp cut |
| **Inclined line** | **1 : 5** from the top of the vertical |
| Shared courts | the façade (or the edge of a veranda / loggia / balcony) must be **≥ 4.50 m** from the opposite boundary of the shared court |
| Exclusion | the width of **watercourses, canals or adjoining public property is NOT counted** in the court's envelope computation |

> [!tip] ★ Definition — the legal court (الفسحة القانونية)
> «تعتبر الفسحة قانونية … عندما لا تقل مساحتها عن ٣٠م٢ وتستوعب مستطيلاً قياساته الدنيا ٤٫٥٠ × ٥٫٥٠م»
> **Area ≥ 30 m²** AND it must **contain a rectangle of at least 4.50 × 5.50 m**. Both tests, not either.
>
> A court is **independent** as long as it does not connect to another court, **or** where it connects over a width of **less than 4.50 m**. At a connection of ≥ 4.50 m the two become **one** court.

> [!warning] The one-metre rule
> Figure 28 carries the condition «يجب أن تكون الفسحة خالية من أي بناء ابتداء من علو متر واحد من مستوى أرض الغرفة المنارة عليها» — *the court must be free of any construction from **1 m above the floor level** of the room lit onto it.*

**Buried basements under shared courts** are permitted beneath courts burdened with a no-build or light-and-air servitude, where this does not conflict with the conditions that created them or with the area's building regulations, subject to Art. 9(2)(د).
