# Lebanon — Permits, Declarations and Exempt Works
### المواد الثانية إلى السادسة · decree pp. 2–26

Parent: [[Lebanon Building Law Implementing Decree 15874-2005]]

## Article 1 — Purpose · p. 2
> «إن غاية هذا المرسوم هي تحديد شروط تطبيق نص مواد القانون رقم ٦٤٦ الصادر بتاريخ ٢٠٠٤/١٢/١١ (قانون البناء).»
One sentence, no sub-clauses.

**Legal basis:** Law 646/2004 (esp. Art. 28) · opinions of the المجلس الأعلى للتنظيم المدني (محضر ٤٤ of 21/09/2005; محضر ٥٢ of 07/11/2005) · Conseil d'État opinions 2004/169-2005 and 2005/6-2006 of 27/10/2005 · Council of Ministers session of 17/11/2005.

## Article 2 — Works exempt from the permit requirement · pp. 2–12

This is the longest of the permitting articles and it is almost entirely about **ground works on sloping land** — boundary walls, retaining walls, fill and excavation. Eighteen of the decree's drawings belong to it.

| Element | Governing limits (from the drawings and text) |
|---|---|
| **التصوينة** — boundary wall, within the setback from roads and public property | h1 ≤ 1.20 m; **h = h1 + h2 ≤ 3 m** (Fig. 2) |
| **التصوينة** outside that setback | h1 ≤ 2 m, h2 ≤ 2 m, **h = h1 + h2 ≤ 3 m** (Fig. 1) |
| Boundary wall above a wall **< 2 m** high | Fig. 3 |
| Boundary wall above a wall **> 2 m** high | h1 > 2 m, h2 ≤ 1 m, **h = h2 + h3 ≤ 2 m** (Fig. 4) |
| **Fill and support walls** within the setback from neighbours | H1 < 2 m, H2 < 3.5 m, H3 < 2 m, H3 < A (Fig. 5) |
| Fill and support walls outside the setbacks | H1 ≤ 3.5 m, H2 ≤ 3.5 m, H3 < A (Fig. 6) |
| Supporting + retaining sections | **H = H1 + H2 ≤ 3.5 m** (Fig. 7) |
| Setback from road or alignment | **4 m ≥ x ≥ 0 m**; a passage 1.2 m wide is not counted in the exploitation (Fig. 8) |
| Excavation and retaining walls | **H ≤ 3.5 m**; a tree every three metres; ≥ 0.8 m (Figs. 9, 10, 11) |
| Plot sloping **above** the road | Figs. 12, 14, 16, 17 |
| Plot sloping **below** road level | slope less than 5% (Fig. 15) |
| Raised sloping ground | **X < Y + 3.5 m + A + B**, **0.30 m < D < 1.20 m**, slope ≤ 30° (Fig. 13) |
| Buildings with sloping roofs | **h = (h1 + 2×h2 + h3) / 4** (Fig. 18) |

> The declaration regime under this article **does not apply** to ordinary maintenance, beautification, restoration and demolition: «لا يطبق نظام التصريح موضوع هذه المادة على أعمال الصيانة العادية والتجميل والترميم والهدم».

## Article 3 — Submitting the declaration · p. 13

The declaration for works under Art. 2(ثانياً)(ب) is lodged with the administration together with the documents fixed by decision of the Minister of Public Works under Art. 3 of the Building Law, and a receipt (الوصل) is issued.

## Article 4 — Building permit procedure and effects · pp. 14–21

Covers the application file, the decision periods, the tacit-approval mechanism where the administration does not answer within the statutory periods of the Building Law, phased permits (المراحل), and the additions regime under Art. 23 of the Building Law.

## Article 5 — Occupancy permit (رخصة الإشغال / السكن) · pp. 22–25

Conditions for the occupancy permit within the meaning of Art. 6 of the Building Law, including **partial occupancy** where a building has been subdivided under Art. 26 of Law 539 of 24/07/1996, and the tacit-approval route where the administration does not answer within the periods of Art. 6 of the Building Law.

## Article 6 — Public and private land remnants (فضلات الأملاك) · p. 26

The price of a remnant is fixed definitively by the committee under **Art. 80 of Decision 275/26**, at the prevailing rate.
