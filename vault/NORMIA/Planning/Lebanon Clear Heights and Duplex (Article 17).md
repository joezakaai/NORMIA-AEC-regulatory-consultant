# Lebanon — Clear Heights and the Duplex
### المادة السابعة عشرة — في الارتفاع الحر تحت السقف · decree pp. 100–101

Parent: [[Lebanon Building Law Implementing Decree 15874-2005]]

## 1 · Minimum clear height — أولاً

| # | Use | Minimum clear height |
|---|---|---|
| ١-أ | Rooms for dwelling or occupation, day or night, or for special activities — **horizontal ceiling** | **2.80 m** |
| ١-ب | Same, **non-horizontal ceiling** | **2.40 m as the average height of the room** |
| ١-ج | **مسكن الناطور** — concierge's dwelling | **2.20 m** |
| ٢ | Buildings and warehouses in the lower floors, and **the garage** | **2.20 m under ceiling and beams** |
| ٣ | **طابق الأعمدة** — pilotis floor | **2.20 m** minimum under ceiling and beams, including the concierge's dwelling within it |
| ٤ | Apartment and office entrances, and kitchens with mezzanines above | **2.20 m** |
| ٥ | Laundry rooms, refuse rooms, bathrooms, WCs, office pantries, corridors | **2.00 m** |
| ٦ | **Duplex** | **2.20 m** in the doubled part |
| ٧-أ | Marine-sport cabins and winter-sport chalets — bedrooms and sitting rooms, **horizontal ceiling** | **2.20 m** |
| ٧-ب | Same, **sloping ceiling** | **1.90 m**, provided the **average height ≥ 2.20 m** and the **slope ≥ 10%** |

## 2 · The duplex (الدوبلكس) — أولاً/٦

> «يُعنى بالدوبلكس الطابق المكون من مستويين، ويدخل كلاهما ضمن عاملَي الاستثمار…»

| Criterion | Value |
|---|---|
| Levels | **2**, and **both count toward both exploitation factors** |
| Total clear height, both levels together / the high part | **≤ 5.75 m** |
| Clear height in the doubled part | **≥ 2.20 m** |
| Area of the **upper** level | **≤ 2/3 of the lower level**, including the internal connecting stair |
| The secondary stair in the FAR | **counted once only** |
| Rooms needing view distance | must give **directly onto the open air, or through the high part of the duplex** |

## 3 · Chalets and cabins — أولاً/٧

| Criterion | Value |
|---|---|
| Horizontal projected area of each chalet dwelling counted in the exploitation factors | **≤ 120 m²** |
| Number of floors | **≤ 2**, excluding the lower floor and the last floor in tile-roof form |
| Where permitted | areas designated **by decision of the المجلس الأعلى للتنظيم المدني** |

## 4 · Maximum clear height — ثانياً

**5.75 m**, except buildings or parts of buildings which by their nature or use require more — subject to the **prior approval of the المجلس الأعلى للتنظيم المدني**.

> [!warning] Source defect
> The source prints «يقترن هذا **الاستثمار** بموافقة…» where the sense requires «**الاستثناء**» (the exception). Apparent typographical error; flagged, not corrected.

## 5 · How height is measured — ثالثاً *(added by Decree 617)*

> «يتم احتساب العلو المنفذ ما بين أسفل السقف ووجه البلاط، ولا تدخل أعمال الزخرفة والديكور في احتساب العلو.»
> Measured **from the underside of the ceiling to the face of the floor tiling**. Decoration and décor works are **not** included.
