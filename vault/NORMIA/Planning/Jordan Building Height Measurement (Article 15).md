# Jordan Building Height Measurement (Article 15)

> [!info] [[NORMIA]] · [[Jordan]] · [[Planning]] · [[Jordan Development Control Table (Article 16)]]

## The definition — Article 2

> **ارتفاع البناء**: "المسافة العامودية بين متوسط منسوب الطريق الى أعلى نقطة من السطح الإنشائي للبناء."
>
> **Building height**: the vertical distance between the **average road level** and the **highest
> point of the structural surface** of the building.

Note what this is not: it is not measured from finished floor level, and not from natural ground
level in the ordinary case. The datum is the **road**.

## Article 15(a) — Plots with more than one adjoining road

| Case | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **1** | Height = vertical distance from the **average of the levels of those roads** to the highest point of the structural surface. **Roads less than 8 m wide are not counted**, nor is a road where the plot frontage on it is **less than 50%** of the length of that frontage. |
| **2** | Where the plot fronts more than one road narrower than in case 1, height is computed from the **average of the levels of those roads**. |
| **3** | Where the plot fronts more than one road and **more than one building** is erected on it, each building's height is computed from the average level of the **road nearest to it**. |

## Article 15(b) — Semi-basement level

With multiple adjoining roads, the level of the **semi-basement (طابق التسوية)** surface is
computed per paragraph (a). **Excluded**: land designated for **ribbon commercial (تجاري طولي)**
and **commercial showrooms (المعارض التجارية)** use, where the semi-basement surface level equals
the **commercial street pavement level**.

## Article 15(c) — Where natural ground is above the road

Where the plot's **natural ground level is higher than the road level**:

1. Height **may** be computed from the **natural ground level**, provided the building contains
   **no semi-basement floors**.
2. The **first floor** may be used for **car parking or services**, and that floor is **excluded**
   from the height and floor-count provisions of this regulation.

## Article 15(d) — Raising the ground slab

The ground floor slab level may be raised by a **maximum of 2 m** above natural ground level or
road level, where the building has **no semi-basement or basement floors**, and provided the
building height does not exceed the permitted limit.

## The floor definitions — Article 2

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **طابق القبو** Basement | The floor(s) below the natural ground level of the plot **on all sides**, provided its concrete surface does not exceed **45 cm** above natural ground level or the average level of the road adjoining the plot, **whichever is lower**. **May be built on the full plot boundary, excluding the front setback.** |
| **طابق التسوية** Semi-basement | The floor(s) below ground floor level whose concrete surface does not exceed **1.5 m** above the average road level. |
| **الطابق الأرضي** Ground floor | The floor or part of a floor directly above natural ground level, or directly above the semi-basement or basement. |

## Design Consequence

**The basement is the most valuable allowance in the Jordanian system**, and it is defined
generously: a true basement may occupy the **entire plot up to the boundary**, excluding only the
front setback. It is also excluded from both the total built area and the floor area (see
[[Jordan Building Regulation 2022]]). On a tight urban plot that is where the parking and services
go, and it costs nothing against the coverage or FAR.

**The 45 cm test is what separates a basement from a semi-basement**, and it is measured against
the *lower* of natural ground or average road level. Miss it and the floor reclassifies as a
تسوية, with a different level rule and different treatment.

**Article 15(c) is a real opportunity on sloping sites.** Where natural ground sits above the road,
electing to measure from natural ground and forgoing the semi-basement gains a parking/services
floor that does not count against height or storey count. That is a design decision to take at
concept, because it forecloses the semi-basement.

**Roads under 8 m do not count toward the average.** On a corner plot served by one wide road and
one lane, the lane is ignored — which can raise or lower the datum materially. Check road widths
before fixing levels.
