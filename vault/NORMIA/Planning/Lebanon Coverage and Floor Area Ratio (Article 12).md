# Lebanon — Coverage and Floor Area Ratio
### المادة الثانية عشرة — معدل الاستثمار السطحي وعامل الاستثمار العام · decree pp. 51–72

Parent: [[Lebanon Building Law Implementing Decree 15874-2005]]

> [!warning] ⚪ The ratios themselves are not in this decree
> **معدل الاستثمار السطحي** (surface exploitation / coverage) and **عامل الاستثمار العام** (general exploitation factor / FAR) are fixed by **each area's own zoning decree (نظام المنطقة)**, not here. Article 12 defines **what counts** toward them — which is where the real design value sits.
> For any given plot the numeric ratios are **UNVERIFIED until the zoning certificate is obtained.**

## What does NOT count — ثانياً

### The buried lower floor (الطابق السفلي المردوم)

| Condition | Limit |
|---|---|
| H1 **outside** the setbacks | **≤ 3.50 m** |
| H1 **within** the setback | **≤ 2.00 m** |
| Stepping in the lower-floor roof | permitted only where **T ≥ 2h** — otherwise not accepted |
| Artificial fill (ردم مصطنع) | **not accepted** |
| Reconstituted ground over the roof | governed by ٢‑أ‑٣ |

### The exposed lower floor (الطابق السفلي المكشوف)

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Exposed part outside the building footprint | **B ≥ 0.6 × L** |
| Where floor count or max height **is** fixed by the zone | Figure 73 |
| Where floor count is **not** fixed | Figures 72, 75 |

### Other exclusions

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Decorative projections, crowning, sun-breakers, cornices, canopies, cladding thickness | excluded (p.62) |
| Electricity transformer rooms | excluded (p.63) |
| Tile-roof sections and insulation works | excluded; eaves conditions at slope **> 25°**, 3 m |
| Transparent roofs over courts | excluded (p.65) |
| External walls / double wall | excluded where **22 cm ≤ e ≤ 35 cm** |
| Stairs and lifts | excluded (p.67) |
| Technical-installation areas | not counted, **not exceeding 20 m²**; X ≥ 1.10 m, Y ≥ 1.40 m |

## What does not count toward FAR **or** the floor count — ثالثاً

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Stair and lift cages and water tanks above the last roof | excluded |
| **مسكن الناطور** — concierge's dwelling | excluded |
| **المتختات** — mezzanines | excluded (clear height limit applies) |
| Technical floors | excluded (pp.69–70) |
| **طابق الأعمدة** — the pilotis floor | excluded, subject to the conditions below |

### The pilotis floor (طابق الأعمدة) — pp. 70–72

| Condition | Value |
|---|---|
| Clear height | **≤ 3.50 m** |
| Level tolerance against the adjoining road or sidewalk | **d ≤ 1 m** and **d' ≤ 1 m** |
| Buildings with a road façade **over 30 m** | the façade is divided — B1, B2, B3 each **< 30 m**, each with its own d **< 1 m** (Fig. 88) |
| Garage roof within the pilotis | **2.30 m** (Fig. 90) |

## Article 13 — Works permitted within the setback
### المادة الثالثة عشرة · p. 73

Within the setback from the road or alignment, and within the setback from neighbouring plots, the decree permits a defined list of works — boundary walls (تصوينة), open stairs, retaining or supporting walls, and a bridge-form building entrance.

## Article 14 — Large complexes (المجموعات الكبرى) · pp. 74–76

Large complexes require a dedicated application file and the prior approval of the **المجلس الأعلى للتنظيم المدني**, with special conditions. **General residential complexes (المجموعات السكنية العامة) are excepted** (خامساً, p.76).
