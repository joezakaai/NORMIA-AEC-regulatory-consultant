# Qatar Car Parking Regulations

> [!info] [[NORMIA]] · [[Qatar]] · [[Planning]] · [[QNMP Zoning Regulation Framework]]
> Ministry of Municipality · **August 2017**

The zone regulations do not state parking ratios — they defer to this document. R1 states:
*"Parking shall be in accordance with the requirements of the Car Parking Regulations."*

## Requirement

Quoted verbatim from the official PDF:

> **Dwellings:** "1 For each one- or two-bedroom dwelling or each 120 Sqm GFA plus 0.25 car
> parking spaces per dwelling for visitors"
>
> "2 For each three or more-bedroom dwelling plus 0.25 car parking spaces per dwelling for visitors"
>
> **Office:** "1 65 Sqm GFA"
>
> **Retail and Services:** "1 40 Sqm GFA"

## Value / Criterion

| Use | Spaces required | Visitor parking |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dwelling — 1 or 2 bedroom | **1** per dwelling, or per 120 m² GFA | **+0.25** per dwelling |
| Dwelling — 3 or more bedroom | **2** per dwelling | **+0.25** per dwelling |
| Office | **1** per **65 m²** GFA | — |
| Retail and services | **1** per **40 m²** GFA | — |

## Design Consequence

A typical family villa (3+ bedrooms) needs **2 spaces plus 0.25 visitor** — so **2.25, rounded
per the document's own rounding rule**, which was not captured and should be checked.

The dwelling ratio has a **dual trigger** — "for each one- or two-bedroom dwelling **or each
120 Sqm GFA**". On a large apartment the GFA limb can bite harder than the bedroom count. Read
the source table before assuming the bedroom count governs.

For mixed-use schemes the office (1/65 m²) and retail (1/40 m²) ratios are materially different;
retail is the most parking-hungry use per square metre and will drive the basement layout.

## Interpretation Notes

This is the **August 2017** edition while the residential zone regulations were reissued in
**April 2020**. The zone regulations point to this document without naming an edition, so a newer
parking edition would automatically apply. **Check the MSDP zoning page for a later edition before
relying on these ratios.**

Accessible parking, loading, bicycle and EV provision were **not captured** from this document and
are recorded as **UNVERIFIED** — read the full PDF.
