# Jordan Parking Requirements (Article 36)

> [!info] [[NORMIA]] · [[Jordan]] · [[Planning]] · [[Jordan Development Control Table (Article 16)]]

Ratios read visually from the gazette scan, page 34.

## Minimum parking by use — Article 36(e)

| Zone / project | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Residential zones (أ، ب، ج، ريفي، زراعي)** | **1 space per 300 m²** of total built area **or 1 space per apartment — whichever is greater** |
| **Residential zone (د)** | **1 space per two apartments** |
| **Commercial zones and complexes** | 1 space per **100 m²** of total built area, **plus** the spaces required for the other varied uses in the complex (cafés, tourist restaurants, theatres, halls and the like) that require additional parking — **whichever is greater** |
| Professions licensed within residential use | **1 space per profession** |
| **Industrial, craft zones and warehouses** | 1 space per **200 m²** of total built area, **plus** 1 space per **50 m²** of the total area of their associated offices |
| **Hotels** | 1 space per **four hotel rooms**, plus the spaces required for other uses including wedding halls and conference rooms, **plus 1 space per hotel suite and per hotel apartment** |
| Places of worship | 1 space per **75 m²** of total built area |
| Nurseries and kindergartens | 1 space per **100 m²** of nursery or kindergarten area |
| Exhibition and conference centres, function halls, wedding halls, showrooms | 1 space per **20 m²** of total built area |
| Ministries, departments and public institutions | 1 space per **20 m²** of total built area |
| Offices, social centres, research centres, veterinary clinics, health and medical centres, clinics and laboratories, banks and financial institutions, private schools and public libraries, museums and civil society institutions, emergency services | 1 space per **50 m²** of total built area |
| Sports clubs and playgrounds, universities, colleges, institutes, sports halls | 1 space per **25 m²** of total built area |
| **Hospitals** | 1 space per **bed and per incubator**, **in addition to** 1 space per **100 m²** of total built area |
| Government schools | 1 space per **100 m²** of total built area |

## Article 36(a) — Exemption

No parking is required for buildings on plots served **only by a stair or a passage less than three
metres wide**, provided the number of apartments in the building does not exceed **six**.

## Article 36(b) — Provision rules

1. **No building may be licensed** unless the parking required for the **entire permitted building**
   on the plot has been provided, regardless of the areas being licensed.
2. Where the parking secured is fewer than required for the whole building, the committee may
   license only the building area or number of apartments matching the **parking actually secured** —
   and **no future licence** may be granted for areas or apartments exceeding that parking.
3. **A fraction of a space counts as one whole space.**
4. A **ground floor designated for car parking is excluded** from the prescribed building height,
   and up to **25%** of its area may be used as general services for the building.
5. **Parking areas in the semi-basement (التسوية) are excluded** from the coverage calculation,
   subject to the structural safety of the building.

## Article 36(c) — Parking in the front setback

**Except in residential zones**, the committee may permit car parking **in the front setback**,
subject to the parking dimensions prescribed in the regulation and to securing free manoeuvring for
entry and exit.

## Article 36(d) — Parking in side and rear setbacks

The committee may accept **uncovered** car parking in the **side and rear setbacks**, subject to:

1. providing the clear width for the parking spaces;
2. securing the front setback behind those spaces and not occupying it in a way that blocks parking
   movement;
3. the minimum entry opening for the parking being not less than its clear width;
4. securing free manoeuvring for entry and exit **within the plot boundaries**.

## Design Consequence

**The residential ratio is a "whichever is greater" test, and the per-apartment limb usually wins.**
1 space per 300 m² is generous; 1 space per apartment is not. A building of six 150 m² apartments is
900 m² — three spaces on the area limb, but **six** on the apartment limb. Always compute both.

**Article 36(b)(1) is the hard gate.** Parking must be provided for the *entire permitted building*,
not just the part being licensed. A phased scheme cannot defer parking to a later phase, and if
parking falls short the licensable area is capped permanently at what the parking supports.

**Two exclusions are worth designing for.** A ground floor given over to parking does not count
against height — and up to a quarter of it can still be building services. Parking in the
semi-basement does not count against coverage. Together these push parking down and out of the
countable envelope, which is usually the right move on a constrained plot.

**Hospitals carry the heaviest load**: a space per bed *and* per incubator, on top of 1 per 100 m².
