# Lusail — Universal Access and Accessible Parking

> [!info] [[NORMIA]] · [[Authority Directory]] · [[Qatar]] · Discipline: [[Accessibility]]
> Source: `Qetaifan Island North District — Planning & Design Guidelines, Residential & Mixed-Use Plots` — REV 2, May 2023, p.28

## Requirement

**§1.5.2, p.28 — Universal Access Design:**

> The aspiration for Lusail City is to maximize opportunities for universal (disabled /
> handicapped) access for all disabilities. All proposals **should provide for barrier-free
> access in accordance with recognized best international practice**. All development proposals
> **will be expected to include and show universal access details** including but not limited to
> access paths, ramps and building entrances.

**§1.5.2, p.28 — Access, Servicing & Parking Design:**

> Plot owners must provide all required parking on site. **At least two parking spaces or 2% of
> the required parking shall be for the disabled.**

**§1.5.2, p.29 — Design of Levels and Drainage:**

> At grade levels within the plots must be designed to integrate well with external levels,
> especially side-walk and street levels **without the requirement for steps and ramps**.

## Value / Criterion

| Parameter | Value | Classification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accessible parking | At least 2 spaces **or** 2% of required parking | MANDATORY (but see note) |
| Barrier-free access standard | "Recognized best international practice" | **UNVERIFIED — no code named** |
| Universal access details on submission | Access paths, ramps, building entrances | MANDATORY |
| At-grade integration with sidewalk/street | Without steps and ramps | MANDATORY |

## Design Consequence

> [!warning] The parking clause does not translate cleanly to a single villa
> "At least two parking spaces or 2% of the required parking shall be for the disabled" is
> drafted for multi-unit and commercial development. Applied literally to a villa plot whose
> total requirement is two spaces, it would make the entire provision accessible — almost
> certainly not the intent.
>
> Treat this as **INFORMATION REQUIRED** for villa typologies rather than as a compliance
> failure, and put the question to CAC at pre-application.

The universal access requirement itself does apply: submissions are expected to show access
paths, ramps and entrances. Read with the levels rule on p.29 — at-grade integration "without
the requirement for steps and ramps" — the practical design move is a level or near-level
threshold at the pedestrian entrance rather than a retrofitted ramp.

**No accessibility code is named anywhere in the document.** "Recognized best international
practice" is not a citable standard.

## Interpretation Notes

Classified MANDATORY for the submission obligation ("will be expected to include and show")
and **UNVERIFIED** for the substantive technical standard, which the source does not identify.

Do not cite ISO 21542, ADA, BS 8300 or any other code as though the Design Guidelines required
it — they do not. As recorded in [[Authority Directory]] §1.2, Qatar has no confirmed single
mandatory national built-environment accessibility code, so the applicable standard must be
agreed with the authority rather than assumed.
