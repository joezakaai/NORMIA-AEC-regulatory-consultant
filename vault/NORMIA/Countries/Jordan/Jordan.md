# Jordan — المملكة الأردنية الهاشمية

> [!info] Part of [[NORMIA]] · [[Authority Directory]] · Template: [[Regulation Record]]

Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan. Development control is national and codified: unlike the Gulf
jurisdictions in this vault, Jordan has **one principal regulation** that sets subdivision
minimums, setbacks, coverage, height, floors and parking for the whole country — subject to local
zoning decisions.

---

## The regulatory stack

| Level | Instrument | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Statute | **قانون تنظيم المدن والقرى والأبنية** — Town, Village and Building Planning Law | Enabling law |
| **Principal regulation** | **[[Jordan Building Regulation 2022]]** — نظام رقم (1) لسنة 2022 | **Zoning + development control + licensing**, 49 pages |
| Amendment | **[[Jordan Amendment Regulation 12 of 2025]]** — نظام رقم (12) لسنة 2025 | Read as **one regulation** with the 2022 |
| Local | **أحكام التنظيم** — local zoning decisions on structural and detailed plans | **Override** the national table where they impose restrictions or grant facilities |
| Professional | **نقابة المهندسين الأردنيين** — Jordan Engineers Association | Certifies engineering plans |

> [!warning] The national table is not the last word
> Article 16(a) of the 2022 regulation opens "**subject to any special provisions in structural or
> detailed plans**". Always obtain the local zoning decision for the plot before relying on the
> national figures.

## Three road-width thresholds — check these first

Jordan gates different entitlements on the width of the serving road. Establish it before any
feasibility conclusion.

| Width | What it unlocks | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **< 8 m** | Road is **not counted** in the height-datum average | Art. 15(a)(1) |
| **≥ 12 m** | Balconies permitted in the front setback | Art. 33 |
| **≥ 12 m** | **Minimum serving street for a project** (renewable energy excepted) | **Art. 10(و), added 2025** |
| **≥ 14 m** | Additional floor of up to 4 m may be licensed | Art. 16(f) |
| **≥ 20 m** | Required to use the high-building table, height bonuses and the 60 m provisions | Art. 16(e) |
