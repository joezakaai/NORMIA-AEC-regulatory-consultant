## Standing caveats

> [!warning] Two things this decree does **not** give you
> **1 · The zone's height and ratios.** نظام المنطقة sits above this decree. The envelope of Article 7 can never exceed the zone's maximum height, and the numeric values of **معدل الاستثمار السطحي** (coverage) and **عامل الاستثمار العام** (FAR) come from the area's own zoning decree, not from Article 12. Article 12 defines only **what counts** toward them.
> **2 · Any plot-specific answer.** ⚪ For a real project both of the above are **UNVERIFIED until the zoning certificate for that plot is obtained.**
