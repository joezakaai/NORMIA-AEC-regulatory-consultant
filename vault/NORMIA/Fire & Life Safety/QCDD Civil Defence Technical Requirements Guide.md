# QCDD Civil Defence Technical Requirements Guide

> [!info] [[NORMIA]] · [[Qatar]] · [[Fire & Life Safety]] · [[Authority Directory]]
> **General Directorate of Civil Defence, Ministry of Interior** · Launched **3 March 2022**
> Foreword signed by **Brigadier Hamad Othman Al-Dehaimi, Director-General of Civil Defence**
> **Free download from the official MOI portal.**

## Scope — verbatim

> "This document provides the minimum fire and life safety requirements for buildings and
> structures of various occupancies prescribed by the General Directorate of Civil Defense (GDCD)."

## Supersession — verbatim

> "This document is an updated version of the Fire Safety Guidelines and Annex published in 2015
> (FSG_2015), and further supersedes and replaces that previous FSG_2015 documents."

## Code basis — NFPA, not IBC

Verbatim from the guide:

> "This document also adopts the National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) codes and standards
> as principal reference for provisions or requirements not specifically addressed in this document."

**Conflict hierarchy — verbatim:**

> "In the event of any conflict concerning requirements under this document and requirements under
> NFPA, the requirements under this document shall be uphold."

**Other codes — verbatim:**

> "Adoption of requirements from other fire and engineering codes and standards should have the
> prior concurrence of GDCD before its application."

NFPA standards cited in the 2022 guide: **NFPA 5000, 101, 70, 110, 111, 96, 45, 30, 54, 55, 58,
400, 495.**

> [!warning] The model building code is NFPA 5000, **not the IBC**
> Neither the 2022 guide nor the 2015 guidelines mention the International Building Code. Claims
> that QCDD is "based on IBC" are **not supported** by the primary documents. Do not design or
> specify to IBC on that assumption.

**NFPA edition years adopted by the 2022 guide were not established** — UNVERIFIED. The 2015
document gave one explicitly (**NFPA 5000, 2012 Ed.**). Since drawing title blocks must state
"applicable codes with complete editions and dates", this must be confirmed from the guide's own
reference list.

## Occupancy chapters

Assembly (Masjid/Mosque; General Assembly) · Educational (Nurseries; Educational) · Health Care
(Ambulatory; Health Care) · Residential (**Hotels, Dormitories, Accommodations, Apartments**) ·
Mercantile (Retail, Malls, Bulk Merchandising) · Business · Industrial (incl. Aircraft Hangars) ·
Storage (incl. **Parking Structures**, Aircraft Storage)

## 🔴 The villa gap — the most significant finding

> [!danger] The 2022 guide contains no chapter on villas, dwellings, or one- and two-family homes
> The 2015 guidelines **did** cover villas, with a dedicated occupancy table. The 2022 guide states
> it "supersedes and replaces" FSG_2015 — but does not replace that content.
>
> Whether villas are now (a) outside QCDD scope, (b) still governed by the 2015 tables despite
> supersession, or (c) covered by an unpublished internal circular **could not be determined from
> any official source.**
>
> **Do not rely on the 2015 villa figures without confirming with GDCD.** Equally, do not assume
> villas are exempt — the municipal permit process routes "life safety" drawings to QCDD at DC-1
> with no stated villa carve-out.

For the record, the superseded **2015** villa provisions were:

| Item | 2015 value (SUPERSEDED — do not apply without confirmation) |
| --- | --- |
| Occupancy | "Villa units / terrace house and town houses (containing less than three or more dwelling units)" |
| Height / area limits | "No requirements" |
| Firebreak to property line | "1.50M" |
| Distance from lot boundary | "1.50M" |
| Construction | "1-HR FRR" for walls between units and exterior bearing walls |
| Detection | Manual / stand-alone smoke alarms **required** |
| Extinguishers | **Required** · Fire blankets in kitchens **required** |
| Standpipe, hose reels, sprinklers | **Not required** |

⚠️ The 1.50 m figures are **property-line setbacks**, not separation between a main villa and an
ancillary building on the same plot. **No clause addressing main-dwelling-to-ancillary separation
was found anywhere** — and no fire-rated kitchen separation requirement for dwellings beyond the
fire blanket.

## Legal basis — a common claim corrected

The governing statute is **Law No. 13 of 1997 with respect to the Civil Defence**, amended by
**Law No. 9 of 2012**.

> [!warning] The widely-cited consultancy claim that QCDD operates under "Law 9/1993" is **not
> corroborated** by the Al Meezan legal index and appears to be wrong.

Statutory text could not be fetched — article-level content is **UNVERIFIED**.
