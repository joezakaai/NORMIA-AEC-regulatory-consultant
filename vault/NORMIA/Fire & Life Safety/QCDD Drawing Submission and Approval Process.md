# QCDD Drawing Submission and Approval Process

> [!info] [[NORMIA]] · [[Qatar]] · [[Fire & Life Safety]] · [[QCDD Civil Defence Technical Requirements Guide]]

> [!warning] Source caveat
> **FF-01-2014-Rev03** is a QCDD-authored document, but it could only be located on a **third-party
> mirror**, not on an moi.gov.qa URL. Content is reported as found; obtain the official copy from
> QCDD before relying on it contractually.

## Where QCDD sits in the permit chain

Confirmed from an official QatarEnergy procedure governing building permits in Mesaieed Industrial
City (*IMM-ECP-GDL-012, Rev01 Nov 2020*):

| Stage | QCDD role — verbatim |
| --- | --- |
| **DC-1** (preliminary / architectural) | Table-1 lists **"Life Safety"** drawings as requiring QCDD approval. *"it will transfer the building permit application through the MME's e-BP system to QCDD for approval of life safety drawings."* |
| **DC-2** (detailed services) | QCDD approvals required for **"Fire Fighting, Fire Alarm, Ventilation."** |
| **Completion Certificate** | *"the online CC application is sent to QCDD for review and inspection of fire engineering related issues."* |

> [!important] Life-safety drawings are approved at DC-1, not at permit stage
> This is the single most consequential programme fact in this record. The fire strategy, egress
> and compartmentation must be resolved during **schematic design**, with the QCDD submission
> running in parallel with the architectural approval — not after it.

That procedure also references *"QCD Fire & Life Safety Guidelines and Annexes"*, *"Fire Code
(NFPA 1)"* and *"Life Safety Code (NFPA 101)"*.

## Who may submit — verbatim (FF-01-2014-Rev03)

> "All drawings, calculations & related design reports shall be signed and endorsed by the
> designated Consultant Engineer, attested by the Engineering Company to where he/she is employed."

> "Drawings to be submitted through MOI Online Permit System Portal must be in ACAD file format"

Site plans and elevation/sectional drawings must be *"scaled to fit an A0 or A1 drawing sheet."*

## Required submissions

- Detailed Design Report — building description, occupancies, fire protection systems
- Floor plans, elevations, sections, miscellaneous details, riser diagrams
- **Policy Plan showing location, PIN and QARS numbers**
- **UPDA registration documentation** for the consultant and the engineering company
- Guidelines checklist

## Title block requirements

Must carry: space for the **QCD stamp** · applicable codes **with complete editions and dates** ·
consultant/contractor firm logo, stamp, address and contacts · owner details · project address,
**PIN**, area, plot and street numbers · plan scale · complete engineer name · revision history ·
consistent sheet numbering.

> [!tip] The PIN links the fire submission to the cadastre
> The Policy Plan and title block both require the plot **PIN** — the same identifier queryable in
> the national cadastre. See [[Qatar GIS — Query Recipes]] recipe 2.

## Equipment

> "equipment and all devices to be used for fire fighting shall be Listed for such purposes and
> approved type by QCDD."

## Two registration regimes

| Regime | Administered by | Governs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **UPDA engineer / consulting office accreditation** | **Ministry of Municipality**, under Law No. (19) of 2005 | Who may **sign and submit** drawings |
| **Fire protection company registration** | **QCDD / Ministry of Interior** | Who may **supply, install and maintain** fire systems |

The official Hukoomi service *"Register Company Operating in Fire Protection Field"* confirms the
second: companies need *"necessary approval to provide fire protection requests and services"* and
*"should have a valid registration in the establishment system"* first.

**There is no evidence of a separate QCDD registration for design consultants** — QCDD requires
UPDA documentation instead.

## Interpretation Notes

The five-stage municipal process described in FF-01-2014 (Enquiry → Sketches → Preliminary Permit →
Final Approval → Building Permit) **dates from 2014**. Qatar has since moved to the **MME e-BP
system** and an AI-assisted permit system. **Re-confirm the current stage names and sequence with
the Ministry of Municipality** before planning a programme around them.

The QatarEnergy procedure is the **Mesaieed Industrial City variant** — it confirms the general
DC-1 → DC-2 → Completion structure, but its QatarEnergy-specific pre-review steps do not apply to
ordinary municipal projects.
