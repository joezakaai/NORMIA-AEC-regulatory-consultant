# QIN — Ancillary Fire Hazard Uses and Civil Defence

> [!info] [[NORMIA]] · [[Authority Directory]] · [[Qatar]] · Discipline: [[Fire & Life Safety]]
> Source: `Qetaifan Island North District — Planning & Design Guidelines, Residential & Mixed-Use Plots` — REV 2, May 2023, p.33, 34, 98

## Requirement

**§2.4.2, p.98 (Sheet 7/7), Key Parameter B:**

> Ancillary buildings may abut side walls and maintain a 2 meters separation from the main
> villa. **Fire hazard uses like kitchens should be referenced to local Civil Defense code
> requirements and other international standards that mandate proper fire-resistant construction
> between such uses and the main villa.**

**§1.6.4 Step 4 (DC-1), p.33:**

> **Initial fire safety approvals are also required at this stage from Qatar Civil Defense
> Department** as well as Electrical and Water supply approval from KM department.

**§1.6.4 Step 6 (Services Review), p.34:**

> … **MoI, QCDD will advise on CCTV and civil defense.**

## Value / Criterion

| Requirement | Value | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ancillary separation from main villa | 2.00 m | Minimum |
| Fire-resistant construction between fire-hazard uses and main villa | Required | **Standard not named** |
| QCDD initial fire safety approval | Required at **DC-1** | Before DC-1 can be finalised |
| QCDD / MoI advice on CCTV and civil defence | Services Review (Step 6) | |

## Design Consequence

**Programme.** QCDD approval is required at **DC-1**, not at building permit stage — alongside
the first architectural approval. Fire strategy, means of escape and any fire-rated separation
must therefore be resolved during schematic design, with the QCDD submission running in
parallel with the QP/CAC architectural review rather than after it. Teams that treat civil
defence as a permit-stage activity lose weeks here.

**Design.** An outside kitchen, or any other fire-hazard use placed in an ancillary block,
triggers fire-resistant construction between that use and the main villa, on top of the 2 m
separation. Since ancillary buildings may abut the side boundary walls, that fire separation
detail has to work at a boundary condition.

> [!warning] The Design Guidelines name no standard
> The text says "local Civil Defense code requirements and other international standards"
> without identifying either. **No fire rating, separation distance or construction type is
> specified.** The applicable requirement must be obtained from QCDD directly — see
> [[Authority Directory]] §1.1. Do not assume a rating.
