# Lusail — Services Review and Utility Authority Approvals

> [!info] [[NORMIA]] · [[Authority Directory]] · [[Qatar]] · Discipline: [[Utilities]]
> Source: `Qetaifan Island North District — Planning & Design Guidelines, Residential & Mixed-Use Plots` — REV 2, May 2023, p.29, 31, 34

## Requirement

**§1.6.3, p.31 — Relevant Authorities and Approvals:**

> Nothing in these Design Guidelines and Controls shall relieve the Developer of the
> responsibility for also meeting the technical requirements of, and securing relevant
> approval(s), NOC(s) or permit(s) from, any government agency … including but not limited to:
> **MMUP · Al Daayen Municipality · Karahmaa · Ashgal · Ooreedoo · Marafeq (SNG, District
> Cooling, Vacuum Waste) · The Department of Civil Defense · Ministry of the Environment ·
> Civil Aviation · Ministry of the Interior (MoI) · GORD**
>
> **Written evidence of all such approvals, permits, No Objection Certificates (NOCs), or other
> permissions (if required) must be submitted to CAC in advance of construction.**

**§1.6.4 Step 6 — Services Review, p.34:**

> At this stage the consultants will obtain approvals from relevant agencies for **electrical,
> telecommunications, water & irrigation and drainage**. Marafeq engineers will advise on **gas,
> district cooling, waste collection** and MoI, QCDD will advise on CCTV and civil defense …
> **All authorizations shall be obtained from relevant agencies prior to DC-2 submittal.**

**§1.5.2, p.29 — Design of Levels and Drainage:**

> **All plot generated surface water run-off, storm drainage and roof drainage must be disposed
> off within the site boundaries** and not directed into adjacent roads or properties or beach,
> or into the sanitary sewer system.

**§1.5.2, p.29 — Integration of Services:**

> It is expected that all service connections design will be to a high quality **so that
> services are not visible** and do not affect quality or appearance of the site or building.

## Design Consequence

The sequencing is what to build the programme around: **all utility authorisations must be
obtained before DC-2 submittal**, and KAHRAMAA electrical and water approval is needed as early
as **DC-1**. Utility approvals are not a downstream activity — they gate the architectural
approval chain.

**Marafeq is distinctive to Lusail and easy to overlook.** It covers gas, district cooling and
**vacuum waste collection**. Vacuum waste affects the refuse strategy and the refuse alcove at
the boundary wall; district cooling removes both the need and the option for conventional
plant, which changes the roof and plant room design. Engage Marafeq before the services concept
is fixed.

**The drainage rule is absolute.** All surface, storm and roof water must be disposed of within
the plot — not to the road, the neighbours, the beach or the foul sewer. That means soakaways
or storage sized within the remaining ground, competing for the same ground as any basement and
the soft landscape minimum.

## Interpretation Notes

**Version-control flag: the Design Guidelines list "MMUP"** — the Ministry of Municipality and
Urban Planning. That ministry no longer exists under that name; it was reorganised in 2021 into
the **Ministry of Municipality** and the **Ministry of Environment and Climate Change** (see
[[Authority Directory]] §5). DG Rev 2 is dated May 2023 and therefore carries an obsolete
authority name, as does its reference to the "Ministry of the Environment".

This does not invalidate the requirement — it means the correspondent is the Ministry of
Municipality. Flagged because using the obsolete name in submissions causes avoidable delay.
