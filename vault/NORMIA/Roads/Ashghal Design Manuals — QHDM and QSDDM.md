# Ashghal Design Manuals — QHDM and QSDDM

> [!info] [[NORMIA]] · [[Qatar]] · [[Roads]] · [[Utilities]] · [[Authority Directory]]

## Qatar Highway Design Manual (QHDM)

> [!danger] Two editions are concurrently live — 2015 **and** 2021
> Confirmed from Ashghal's own IAN, whose title reads *"Ashghal – Amendment to Qatar Highway
> Design Manuals (QHDM) **2015 & 2021**"* (PWA IAN 044, Rev 1, June 2021). The IAN states it
> *"shall be read in conjunction with"* **both** versions.

**There is no single "current edition".** Compliance requires reading the manual **plus** the live
IAN register.

**Why it binds the architect at the plot boundary.** IAN 044 Rev 1 amends exactly the plot-edge
clauses: **Volume 1 Part 3** (Road Design Elements — Cross-Section Elements, **Parking Bays**) and
**Volume 1 Part 6** (Design for Priority Intersections — **Visibility**, **Corner Radii**).

Those govern:

- **driveway and crossover location** at the plot boundary
- **sight lines / visibility splays** across the plot corner
- **on-plot parking bay dimensions**

A villa or building entrance designed without checking the current visibility splay can fail at
permit even though the building itself complies with the zoning regulation.

## Qatar Sewerage and Drainage Design Manual (QSDDM)

Issued by the **Public Works Authority, Drainage Affairs**.

> [!warning] A 2023 Ashghal document still normatively cites a **June 2005** volume
> Verbatim from PWA IAN 048/14 Rev 0, dated **August 2023**:
>
> > "thrust force considered in structural design shall be calculated based on Maximum Design
> > Pressure (MDP) and System Test Pressure (STP) as defined in **Qatar Sewerage and Drainage
> > Design Manual, Volume 4: TSE System Design (June 2005)**, Clause 2.7.3"
>
> So the 2005-era QSDDM remains live, as amended by IANs. Do not assume an old date means a
> superseded document.

**Volume structure — partially established:**

| Volume | Title | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vol 1 | General | Third-party listing only |
| Vol 4 | **TSE System Design (June 2005)** | ✅ Confirmed official |
| **Vol 8** | **Developers Guide** | ⚠️ Third-party listing only — **the volume most relevant to a building designer at the plot interface** |

**Full volume list and current edition of each: UNVERIFIED.**

## Interim Advice Notes — the amendment mechanism

IANs continuously amend both manuals. **Always check the IAN register, not just the manual.**

⚠️ **IAN numbering is inconsistent and unreliable as a date cue** — IAN 048/14 is marked "Rev 0"
yet dated **August 2023**. **Date-check every IAN individually.**
