# Lusail — Vehicle Access, Parking Geometry and Site Levels

> [!info] [[NORMIA]] · [[Authority Directory]] · [[Qatar]] · Discipline: [[Roads]]
> Source: `Qetaifan Island North District — Planning & Design Guidelines, Residential & Mixed-Use Plots` — REV 2, May 2023, p.28, 29

## Requirement

**§1.5.2, p.28 — Access, Servicing & Parking Design:**

> **Vehicle access to plots and building plots is permitted only from the access roads and points
> indicated in the Plot Building Regulation sheets** unless otherwise agreed with Master Developer
> and subject relevant Authority standards and permits.
>
> Access and service areas for delivery, garbage collection and other service traffic **should be
> separated from other traffic movement**.
>
> Plot owners must provide all required parking on site.
>
> Typical Parking spaces are to be **minimum 2.65m x 5.8m** with **minimum aisle width for
> perpendicular parking to be 6.7m** unless otherwise agreed and subject to Authority requirements.
>
> Surface Parking areas shall be landscaped to a high quality and should include **adequate
> shading** to parking spaces. All parking areas are to connect with the local pedestrian path
> system.
>
> Parking provision shall also be made for access and parking of **bicycles, motorbikes, and
> motorcycles** in appropriate locations.

**§1.5.2, p.29 — Design of Levels and Drainage:**

> All development proposals will be expected to **integrate seamlessly into the levels of their
> surroundings** and meet relevant Authority standards and permits. **Site and context levels must
> be carefully checked and referenced.**

## Design Consequence

**Vehicular access is a given, not a design variable.** It may only be taken from the point
shown on the plot's own Building Regulation Sheet — which in turn fixes the boundary wall gate
position, the canopy, and the entrance drainage channel location.

The parking dimensions are worth checking against the villa's garage or car shade early:
2.65 × 5.80 m per space with a 6.70 m aisle is generous, and a car shade sized to the 6.00 m
maximum depth permitted in the neighbour side setback will not accommodate a 5.80 m space plus
manoeuvring without careful planning.

**Site and context levels "must be carefully checked and referenced".** This connects directly
to the height controls: because maximum building height is measured **from the lowest side of
the plot** (see [[QIN Villa Type B — Height, Floors and Level Controls]]), the surrounding road
and plot levels must be established before the section can be fixed. Levels drawings are issued
by the master developer as site-wide streetscape level plans; obtain the sheet covering the plot
and confirm the datum before setting finished floor level.

## Interpretation Notes

"Adequate shading" to parking is not quantified in the source. Confirm the expectation with QP's reviewer rather than assuming a percentage.
