# QIN Front Boundary Wall — Heights

> [!info] [[NORMIA]] · [[Authority Directory]] · [[Qatar]] · Discipline: [[Developer Guidelines]]
> Source: `Front Boundary Wall — VL-01 + VL-02 Unification of Boundary Wall Design` — REV 00, issued November 2025, p.4, 5, 6

## Requirement

**Front Boundary Wall — VL-01 + VL-02, KEY PARAMETERS:**

> **A — Front Boundary Wall Height: Maximum 2.5 meters.**
> **B — Canopy Height: Maximum 3.7 meters to top; minimum 3.0 meters clear height below.**
> **C — Ancillary Structures Allowed (accessible) for: Driver's guard room, refuse alcove,
> garbage room.**
> **D — Ancillary Structure Height: Maximum 3.5 meters to top of parapet.**
> **E — Party Wall Height (First 5m): Maximum 2.5 meters.**
> **F — Party Wall Height (Beyond 5m): From 2.5 meters up to Maximum 3.0 meters.**

**Scope:**

> The establishment of a standardized guideline and construction details for boundary wall
> design are intended to the two (2) villa precinct: **North Villa Precinct · East Villa
> Precinct**
> North Villa Precinct — approximate 3193 meters in length
> East Villa Precinct — approximate 2022 meters in length

## Value / Criterion

| Element | Maximum height | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Front boundary wall | **2.50 m** | Reduced from the 3.00 m in earlier documents |
| Party (side) wall — first 5 m from front | 2.50 m | Steps up beyond |
| Party (side) wall — beyond 5 m | 2.50 m rising to 3.00 m max | |
| Canopy | 3.70 m to top; 3.00 m min clear below | |
| Ancillary structure | 3.50 m to top of parapet | Driver's/guard room, refuse alcove, garbage room |

## Design Consequence

If this document governs, the boundary wall changes materially from what the Plot Building
Regulation Sheets and the Design Guidelines imply. The front wall drops from 3.00 m to
**2.50 m**, and the side walls must **step** — 2.50 m for the first 5 m from the front
boundary, rising to a maximum of 3.00 m beyond.

That stepped profile is a visible architectural decision, not a detail. It must be set out on
the boundary elevation at DC-1, and it changes the proportions of the gate and canopy
composition.

The 3.50 m ancillary structure height is consistent with the Design Guidelines' 3.5 m service
block figure, so that item is **not** in dispute.

**Do not design a 3.00 m wall throughout on the strength of the parcel sheet alone.**

## Interpretation Notes

A presentation-format guideline, originating as "BOUNDARY WALL.REV00.pptx", PDF created
13 November 2025, marked **REV 00**. It is the most recent instrument in the set by more than
two years, and the Design Guidelines contain a forward reference to exactly this kind of
document: "Perimeter walls and entrances to **comply with boundary wall design guidelines &
controls**. Boundary walls heights are variable due to slope conditions" (DG p.92).

That gives a strong reading that it governs. But it carries no approval signature block
equivalent to a Plot Building Regulation Sheet, and has not been confirmed as formally issued
to plot owners. Confidence **MEDIUM**.
