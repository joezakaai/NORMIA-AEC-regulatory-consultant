# QIN Front Boundary Wall — Gates, Materials and Finishes

> [!info] [[NORMIA]] · [[Authority Directory]] · [[Qatar]] · Discipline: [[Developer Guidelines]]
> Source: `Front Boundary Wall — VL-01 + VL-02 Unification of Boundary Wall Design` — REV 00, issued November 2025, p.7, 8, 9

## Requirement

**Gate Matrix — permitted vehicular gate types:**

> **A Roll-up** (vertical curtain coils into barrel; guided in side tracks) ·
> **B Side-hinge** (single or double leaf pivot) ·
> **C Sliding 50/50** (two leaves slide apart) ·
> **D Tilt-up** (single panel tilts on pivot arms with counterbalance)

**Materials & finish:**

> Structural, trims and frames: **Stainless steel 316L; passivated. Optional PVD coating.**
> **Aluminum panels: 6063-T6**; epoxy primer + TGIC polyester or PVDF; solid color.
> Powder coating: Sandgrain texture in **Dark Grey Metallic, Grey Metallic and Bronze Metallic**.
> Cladding: **High pressure phenolic panel**, ventilated backing and concealed fixings.

**Finish Matrix:**

> Gate / doors: **RAL 7022** Umbra Grey Metallic · **RAL 7013** Brown Grey Metallic ·
> **RAL 7048** Patina Pearl Mouse Grey — Sandgrain Matt, optional 10% gloss
> Wall finish (Stucco Dash / marble / natural stone): **RAL 1013** Oyster white ·
> **RAL 9001** Cream · **RAL 9010** Pure white
> Ironmongery: stainless steel polished · stainless steel brushed · antique brass brushed

**General notes:**

> Lighting Fixture: Contractor to follow lighting temperature color: **2000k**. "Up and down"
> fixture model to follow approved colors and finishes.
> Security service door & Refuse/Garbage Door: Accessible under canopy … **Door leaf shall open
> inward.**
> Pavement underneath canopy: **Floor finish must follow ROW existing pavers** inside the recess
> vehicular gate up-to the Vehicular gate.
> Drainage channel grate: … Design consultant must **locate the drainage at the transition point
> between the ROW and Plot vehicular entrance align within the boundary line.**
> The contractor may select any combination from the finish matrix that best fits the project.

| Item | Specification |

## Design Consequence

A closed specification, not a palette of suggestions. The gate type must be one of four; the
frame alloy, panel alloy, coating system and RAL numbers are all named. Anything outside the
matrix will be rejected at review. There is choice *within* the matrix — the document says the
contractor may select any combination — but the matrix itself is fixed.

Two items carry programme or coordination consequences:

- **Stainless steel 316L with optional PVD** and high-pressure phenolic cladding are long-lead
  specialist items. Identify the supplier before DC-2, not at shop drawing stage.
- **2000 K is a very warm colour temperature.** If the villa's own external lighting scheme
  runs at 2700 K or 3000 K, the boundary will visibly mismatch the house. Coordinate the two
  schemes deliberately rather than discovering it on site.

The entrance drainage channel must sit **on the boundary line** at the ROW/plot transition —
a levels and falls coordination item between the landscape, civil and boundary wall packages.
