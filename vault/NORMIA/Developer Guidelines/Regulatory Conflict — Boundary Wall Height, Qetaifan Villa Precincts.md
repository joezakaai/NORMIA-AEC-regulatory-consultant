# REGULATORY CONFLICT — Boundary Wall Height

> [!danger] 🟠 UNRESOLVED — do not issue boundary wall design until confirmed
> Two instruments in the Qetaifan Island North document set give different maximum boundary
> wall heights for the same villa plots. The document hierarchy does **not** resolve it,
> because the conflicting document post-dates the legal-basis document by three years.

[[NORMIA]] · [[Authority Directory]] · [[Qatar]] · [[Developer Guidelines]]

---

## Requirement A — 3.00 m on all sides

| Document | Edition | Location | Wording |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Plot Building Regulation Sheets** | Issued per plot; sheets seen carry REV 1, 2022 | "BUILDING REGULATIONS" panel, HEIGHT | "BOUNDARY WALL HEIGHT: 3.00m" |
| **QIN Planning & Design Guidelines** | **REV 2, May 2023** | §2.4.2 Villa Type B, p.92 and p.94 (Key Parameter E) | "Boundary wall: 3m on all sides" |

**Weight:** the Plot Building Regulation Sheet is described by the Design Guidelines
themselves (p.5) as providing "**the legal basis for development**", and §1.6.1 (p.30) states
"the Plot Building Regulations are mandatory".

---

## Requirement B — 2.50 m front, stepped party wall

| Document | Edition | Location | Wording |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Front Boundary Wall — VL-01 + VL-02, Unification of Boundary Wall Design** | **REV 00**, PDF created 13 Nov 2025 | KEY PARAMETERS A, E, F | "A — Front Boundary Wall Height: **Maximum 2.5 meters**." · "E — Party Wall Height (First 5m): **Maximum 2.5 meters**." · "F — Party Wall Height (Beyond 5m): From 2.5 meters up to **Maximum 3.0 meters**." |

**Scope:** the document states it is "intended to the two (2) villa precinct: **North Villa
Precinct · East Villa Precinct**" — so villa plots in both precincts are in scope on the
document's own terms.

---

## Why the hierarchy does not resolve it

Normally [[Lusail — Regulatory Hierarchy and Mandatory Status]] settles this: the Plot Building
Regulation Sheet is the legal basis and governs. Three factors block that conclusion.

1. **Recency.** Requirement B post-dates the parcel sheets by roughly three years and the
   Design Guidelines by two and a half. A later instrument from the same developer
   organisation would ordinarily be read as the current position.
2. **The Design Guidelines defer to it.** DG p.92, under ADDITIONAL REGULATIONS, states:
   "**Perimeter walls and entrances to comply with boundary wall design guidelines &
   controls.** Boundary walls heights are variable due to slope conditions." That is an
   explicit forward reference to a separate boundary wall instrument — which is what
   Requirement B appears to be. On this reading B is not a competing document but the one the
   DG intended to govern.
3. **But B lacks the formal markers of an issued regulation.** It originates as a PowerPoint,
   is marked REV 00, and carries no approval signature block equivalent to a Plot Building
   Regulation Sheet. It has not been confirmed as formally issued to plot owners.

The result is a genuine unresolved conflict, not a hierarchy question.

---

## Potential interpretation

The most probable reading is that **Requirement B governs**: it is the specific boundary wall
instrument the Design Guidelines point to, it is the most recent, and its stepped profile is
consistent with the DG's own note that boundary wall heights "are variable". Under that
reading the flat "3.00m" is the general-case maximum and B refines it by position along the
wall.

**This is an interpretation, not a finding. NORMIA does not adopt it.**

---

## Design consequence if unresolved

- Under A: a continuous 3.00 m wall to the road.
- Under B: a 2.50 m front wall, with side walls stepping from 2.50 m up to 3.00 m at 5 m back
  from the front boundary.

Two visibly different pieces of architecture at the street. The stepped profile under B must be
set out on the boundary elevation, changes the gate and canopy proportions (canopy max 3.70 m
to top, 3.00 m clear below), and alters the privacy performance of the front garden.

Committing to A and later being held to B means redesigning and rebuilding the boundary — the
most visible element of the villa from the public realm, and a long-lead specialist package.

**Severity: HIGH.** Not life-safety, but it risks rejection at DC-1/DC-2 and abortive
construction.

---

## Recommended action

> Seek written confirmation from **Qetaifan Projects (QP)** and the **Lusail City
> Administration Complex (CAC)**, who under DG §1.6.3 hold the power to "interpret these Design
> Guidelines and Controls and to clarify any ambiguities contained therein", and whose
> "decision shall be final and binding".

Questions to put in writing before any boundary wall design is issued:

1. Is the November 2025 Front Boundary Wall document (REV 00) formally issued and binding?
2. Does it supersede the 3.00 m boundary wall height on approved Plot Building Regulation
   Sheets?
3. Will revised Plot Building Regulation Sheets be issued to reflect the 2.50 m front wall?
4. Does the stepped party wall profile apply to both side boundaries?
5. How does "boundary walls heights are variable due to slope conditions" (DG p.92) interact
   with these fixed maxima on a sloping plot?

**Until answered, design to the more restrictive reading (2.50 m front) so that either outcome
remains achievable**, and record the assumption on the drawings.
